## §B. Operative terms - VERBATIM (2026-governing)

### B.1 Term / duration

> "commencing with the 2026 MLB Spring Training and during each year of the Term (as defined in the Agreement), Cardinals have requested, and Contractor has agreed to perform, the Florida Services (as defined below)."

[Source: KitchFix Food Services Agreement Jupiter Complex fully executed 10.14.25.pdf § Recitals p.1]

Term inherited from base Agreement (MO § 3.a: through December 31, 2027). Florida Services layer, per section 2(c), extends through November 19, 2027 conditional on work stoppage outcome:

> "Neither Party shall be obligated to continue Florida Services or payments (as applicable) beyond November 19, 2027, unless and until such arrangement is mutually agreed upon in writing."

[Source: KitchFix Food Services Agreement Jupiter Complex fully executed 10.14.25.pdf § 2(c)(vii) p.3]

### B.2 Per-meal rates

NOT PRESENT. The Amendment uses a flat annual fee structure; no per-meal rate is stated. (Meal-count / portion standards appear only as SLA quantities in Exhibit 3 - see B.9.)

### B.3 Service fee (amount, structure, installment schedule verbatim)

> "a. Total Annual Fee: The total annual fee payable to Contractor for the Florida Services is $2,300,000 (the "Total Annual Fee"), which consists of the following:
> i. $1,400,000 for the Florida Services, payable in quarterly installments on the following dates:
> 1. November 1, 2025;
> 2. February 1, 2026;
> 3. May 1, 2026;
> 4. August 1, 2026, and
> 5. In accordance with Section 2(c) hereof for 2027.
> ii. $900,000 as the budget for the cost of food, packaging, and supplies. Contractor will provide the Cardinals with bi-monthly invoices for these expenses, in addition to receipts, and at no time will charge more than the exact costs for said items. Contractor will provide Cardinals with monthly updates on budget tracking, but Contractor shall not be responsible for ensuring the budget limit. Any additional requests, changes in scope, or ingredient modifications may result in costs exceeding the budget estimate. Any savings realized will be solely the Cardinals' benefit."

[Source: KitchFix Food Services Agreement Jupiter Complex fully executed 10.14.25.pdf § 2(a) pp.1-2]

> "b. Ongoing annual upkeep expenses for the Florida Services:
> i. $15,000/year equipment budget and/or potential repair budget of up to $15,000, which shall roll over if unused;
> ii. $4,000/year for storage pod rental; and
> iii. $11,000/year for temporary cooler storage (during Spring Training only) plus necessary electrical hook-ups."

[Source: KitchFix Food Services Agreement Jupiter Complex fully executed 10.14.25.pdf § 2(b) p.2]

**2027 work-stoppage lock-in (verbatim):**

> "c. 2027 Season and Potential Work Stoppage.
> i. The Parties acknowledge the possibility of a work stoppage during the 2027 Major League Baseball season, which may limit or make unnecessary the Florida Services.
> ii. The Cardinals shall pay Contractor the first quarterly Florida Services installment of the Total Annual Fee for 2027, in the amount of $350,000 plus applicable taxes, on November 1, 2026. Such payment shall be deemed earned in full by Contractor and shall cover Contractor's fixed costs and readiness obligations for the period of January 1 through March 31, 2027, regardless of whether the Florida Services are performed during such period, in whole or in part.
> iii. If any work stoppage continues beyond March 31, 2027, and no Florida Services are being provided by Contractor at Roger Dean Chevrolet Stadium, then the Cardinals shall pay to Contractor on April 1, 2027, a standby installment equal to fifty percent (50%) of the second quarterly Florida Services installment (i.e., $175,000 plus applicable taxes).
> iv. If, however, any Florida Services are provided by Contractor after March 31, 2027 (including, without limitation, Florida Services to Minor League players or other personnel), then the Cardinals shall pay Contractor the full second quarterly Florida Services installment (i.e., $350,000 plus applicable taxes) on April 1, 2027, subject to the parties making an equitable adjustment to account for reduced quantities and/or quality of food and beverage service in absence of any Major League players at the at Roger Dean Chevrolet Stadium for any duration during such period.
> v. Upon conclusion of the work stoppage (if after March 31, 2027), if the Cardinals had made only a standby installment pursuant to subsection (ii), then the Cardinals shall immediately pay the remaining balance of the second quarterly Florida Services installment, and Contractor shall resume Florida Services within ten (10) days of written notice of the work stoppage's conclusion.
> vi. For clarity, once Florida Services resume in 2027, the Cardinals shall thereafter make quarterly payments of the Florida Services portion of the Total Annual Fee on July 1, 2027, and October 1, 2027.
> vii. If the work stoppage continues beyond June 30, 2027, the Parties agree that the payment structure described above shall no longer apply, and the Parties shall promptly meet and confer in good faith to determine an equitable arrangement for the balance of the 2027 season. Neither Party shall be obligated to continue Florida Services or payments (as applicable) beyond November 19, 2027, unless and until such arrangement is mutually agreed upon in writing."

[Source: KitchFix Food Services Agreement Jupiter Complex fully executed 10.14.25.pdf § 2(c) pp.2-3]

### B.4 Escalation clause

NOT PRESENT in Amendment. The Amendment states a fixed $2,300,000 Total Annual Fee with no CPI or price-adjustment mechanic for Florida Services. The base Agreement's CPI clause (see MO §B.4) governs the Missouri-side services only by its terms ("Pricing for 2026 will be adjusted ..."); the Amendment does NOT extend that clause to the Florida Services fee.

[Source: KitchFix Food Services Agreement Jupiter Complex fully executed 10.14.25.pdf § 5 pp.3-4]
