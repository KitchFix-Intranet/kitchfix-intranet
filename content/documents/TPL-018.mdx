# Purpose

The consolidated daily log every KitchFix kitchen fills in to satisfy SOP-008 Food Safety Management. One log per site per day. Every threshold below derives from SOP-008; nothing here is invented. The PIC owns the log during the day; the Executive Chef verifies at end of day.

> ANCHOR: One log, one day, one site. The blank log structure is the canonical record; filled-in readings are operator data and live on the printed/used copy, not in this source.

# How to use

- Complete the header at the start of shift.
- Record AM temperatures on equipment before opening.
- Log each receiving, cooking, cooling, holding, reheating event as it happens (do not batch at end of day).
- Calibrate thermometers at start of day or when dropped.
- Run sanitizer checks per the warewashing schedule.
- PIC signs at end of day; Executive Chef verifies.
- Any out-of-range reading triggers a corrective-action entry in section 10.

# Header (top of every daily sheet)

- Account / site
- Date
- Person in Charge (PIC) on duty
- Executive Chef (verifying)
- Shift / day-part (if the site runs multiple)
