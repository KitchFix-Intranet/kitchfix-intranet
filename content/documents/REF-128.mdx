### B.1 Term / duration

> "**Term of this SOW.** The term of this SOW will commence on April X, 2019 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") and will continue until December 1, 2019 (the "**SOW Term**") unless terminated earlier pursuant to subsection 5(c) of Agreement. *Source: 2019.04.16_Buffalo_Catering_SOW_v1.0_w_3rdpartyvendor.docx.pdf Schedule "E" § 2 p.2*

### B.2 Per-meal rates

> "**Per Meal Compensation**. As compensation for the Catering Services, the Club shall be required to pay the Provider at the following rate (the "**Per Meal Rate**"), plus applicable taxes: Eighteen dollars and Seventy Five cents (USD $18.75) per Meal prepared and served by the Provider in accordance with this SOW."

*Source: 2019.04.16_Buffalo_Catering_SOW_v1.0_w_3rdpartyvendor.docx.pdf Schedule "E" § 9 p.4*

Underlying scale (draft note):

> "**Initial Per-Regular Season Home Game Meal Order**. It is the currently the intention of the parties that the Provider will prepare two (2) Meals for each of forty (40) persons (total eighty (80) Meals) in respect of each of the seventy (70) regular season Home Games scheduled to be played during the SOW Term (the "**Initial Per-Regular Season Home Game Meal Order**") and the Provider will proceed on this basis unless otherwise notified by the Club. In the event that the INT Team qualifies to participate in any Post Season Home Games, the Club will notify the Provider of the necessary order information. [**Note to Draft**: This contains the assumption that the Provider will provide for all 70 home games. It will have to be updated to correct for the more limited number of games for which services will in fact be provided.]"

*Source: 2019.04.16_Buffalo_Catering_SOW_v1.0_w_3rdpartyvendor.docx.pdf Schedule "E" § 4(b) p.3*

In the event that the INT Team qualifies for any Post Season Home Games during the SOW Term, the Provider shall invoice the Club on a bi-weekly basis for all Meals ordered, prepared and served in connection with Post Season Home Games during the period since the last bi-weekly invoice."

### B.8 Billing cadence / payment terms

Regular season: 10 semi-monthly (May 1 - Sep 15) installments of $8,295 after a $22,050 prepayment (§ 10(b)). Postseason: bi-weekly invoicing (§ 11). Reconciliation via Regular Season Report + Regular Season Invoice within 5 business days of season end (§ 10(c)(i)-(ii)):

> "**Regular Season Report**. Within five (5) Business Days following the conclusion of the final Regular Season Home Game, the Provider shall submit to the Club a detailed report (the "**Regular Season Report**") setting out the actual number of Meals ordered by the Club and prepared and served by the Provider, broken down by Service Date, along with a calculation of the total amount payable for those Meals, using the Per Meal Rate (the "**Actual Total Regular Season Payment Amount**")..."

*Source: 2019.04.16_Buffalo_Catering_SOW_v1.0_w_3rdpartyvendor.docx.pdf Schedule "E" § 10(c)(i) p.6*

> "**Regular Season Invoice**. Along with the Regular Season Report, the Provider shall deliver to the Club an invoice for the Actual Total Regular Season Payment Amount. The invoice shall confirm that the Club has already made payment of an amount equal to the Regular Season Estimated Aggregate Payment. In the event that the Actual Total Regular Season Payment Amount exceeds the Regular Season Estimated Aggregate Payment, the Provider shall forthwith refund the amount of the overpayment to the Club. In the event that the invoice indicates an amount owing by the Club, the Club shall pay the amount in accordance with subsection 11(c) of this SOW."

*Source: 2019.04.16_Buffalo_Catering_SOW_v1.0_w_3rdpartyvendor.docx.pdf Schedule "E" § 10(c)(ii) p.6*

> "Except where a specific timing commitment is otherwise stated in this SOW, the Club will make reasonable efforts to make payment of compliant, undisputed invoices by the due date specified on the invoice. For certainty, notwithstanding the foregoing, the Club does not guarantee payment in less than forty five (45) days from receipt of the invoice."

*Source: 2019.04.16_Buffalo_Catering_SOW_v1.0_w_3rdpartyvendor.docx.pdf Schedule "E" § 12(c) p.6*
