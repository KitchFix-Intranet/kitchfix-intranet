# The Four Levels (plus PIP)

| Level | When to Use | Form | Notify |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 - Verbal Coaching | First minor issue, first coaching moment | FORM-003 (manager signs only) | RDO - same day |
| 2 - Written Warning | Recurrence after Level 1, or more serious first-time issue | FORM-004 | RDO - same day, HR within 24hrs |
| 3 - Final Written / Suspension | Recurrence after Level 2, or serious single incident | FORM-004 (marked Final) | RDO approval BEFORE issuing. HR notified. |
| PIP - Performance Improvement Plan | Leadership-track underperformance addressable over <Fact id="pip_standard_days" />. Sits between Level 2 and Level 4 on the leadership track. | FORM-005 | RDO + HR approval BEFORE issuing |
| 4 - Termination | Recurrence after Level 3 or PIP fail, or zero-tolerance violation | FORM-006 | RDO + HR IMMEDIATELY |

# Zero-Tolerance - Immediate Termination

## IMMEDIATE TERMINATION - Zero-Tolerance Violations

- Big Rules violations (AGR-001) - confidentiality breach, media contact, player information disclosure
- Theft - company, client, player, or colleague property
- Fighting or physical altercation on client property
- Reporting to work under the influence, or consuming alcohol/drugs on client property
- Gross food safety violation endangering health - including deliberate contamination or concealment
- Harassment - sexual, discriminatory, or hostile work environment conduct

> CRITICAL: Never terminate without notifying your RDO and HR first. Secure the situation. Then call.

# Always / Never - Manager Rules

| Rule | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Always | Address the issue privately, never in front of the team or clients. |
| Always | Complete the form the same day. Memory fades - write it down while it's fresh. |
| Always | Notify your RDO by end of day for any Level 1+. Text is fine for Level 1. Call for Level 2+. |
| Always | Submit forms to People Operations within 24 hours. |
| Always | State the standard clearly. "You were out of dress code" is not enough. "Your uniform had a visible stain and I asked you to change before service" is. |
| Never | Skip levels without RDO approval. Jumping straight to termination without documentation is how you create legal exposure. |
| Never | Make promises about outcomes. "This is your last warning" is fine. "You're definitely getting fired next time" is not. |
| Never | Discuss a disciplinary action with other team members. |
| Never | Terminate without notifying your RDO and HR first - even in a zero-tolerance situation. Secure the situation and call. |
