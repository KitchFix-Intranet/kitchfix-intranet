# How to Use

One worksheet per account. Fill before opening the dedicated chat.

## 1.1 Workflow

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Copy this worksheet to a working file named for the account. |
| 2 | Fill in every field below. If a field does not apply, write 'N/A'. Do not leave blank. |
| 3 | Pull the legacy SOP/SLA for this account from the archive. |
| 4 | Open a new Claude chat for this account. |
| 5 | Paste the SLA Rebuild Handoff Prompt as the first message. |
| 6 | Copy your filled Account Context from this worksheet into the prompt's Account Context section. |
| 7 | Attach: PB-005, TPL-014, REF-005-A, REF-005-B, and the legacy SOP. |
| 8 | Send. The chat is now ready to begin Draft 1. |

## 1.2 Why This Matters

The new chat does not know anything about this account when it opens. The quality of Draft 1 depends entirely on how completely this worksheet is filled. A complete worksheet means Draft 1 lands close to the mark. A thin worksheet means Draft 1 will have wide gaps that take additional turns to close.

> ANCHOR: Treat this worksheet as the most important step in the SLA rebuild for each account. Time invested here saves time in every downstream draft and conversation.
