# Allergen Awareness - Posted Summary

A wall-mounted reference for every kitchen. The binding text is PB-002 Allergen Playbook. This posting is the at-a-glance reference for the Top 9 allergens and the reaction response.

## The Top 9 Allergens

The FDA's nine major food allergens. Memorize these. Every kitchen carries this posting.

- **Milk** - butter, cream, cheese, ghee, whey, casein, ranch.
- **Eggs** - mayo, aioli, Caesar dressing, fresh pasta, breading, custard.
- **Fish** - Worcestershire, Caesar dressing (anchovy), fish sauce, surimi.
- **Shellfish** - oyster sauce, shrimp paste, some stock bases.
- **Tree Nuts** - pesto (pine nuts), marzipan, nut oils, crusted proteins.
- **Peanuts** - PB, satay sauce, energy bars, cold-pressed peanut oil.
- **Wheat** - bread, pasta, soy sauce, teriyaki, breading, roux, croutons.
- **Soy** - soy sauce, teriyaki, tofu, edamame, miso, vegetable oil (soybean), lecithin.
- **Sesame** - tahini, hummus, sesame oil, everything seasoning, halva.

## The Anaphylaxis Four

The four allergens with the shortest response window when reaction occurs: **Fish, Shellfish, Tree Nuts, Peanuts**. A reaction to any of these can become life-threatening in minutes.

## We Do Not Offer Allergen-Free Meals

KitchFix does not offer allergen-free meals or stations. We label accurately, prevent cross-contact, communicate transparently, and let players make informed decisions.

## If Someone Has a Reaction - Six Steps

1. **Get medical help immediately** - contact on-site medical. If severe (breathing difficulty, swelling, loss of consciousness): call 911. Stay with the person.
2. **Remove the food** - pull the suspected item and all utensils. Preserve packaging and remaining product.
3. **Stabilize** - clean the station, replace equipment, ensure safe service can continue.
4. **Notify within 10 minutes** - Client and KitchFix leadership. Severity 1 phone-tree per SOP-002 section 6.
5. **Document same day** - file the incident report in the People Portal before end of shift.
6. **Follow up within 24 hours** - reconnect with the Client. Share findings, corrective actions, retraining plan.

## Where to find the full Allergen Playbook

The binding text, the daily non-negotiables (labeling, cross-contact, vendor substitution, storage, peanut/tree nut standard), and Site Leader responsibilities live in PB-002 Allergen Playbook.
