# Billing Model Quick Reference

**Purpose.** Answer "what is the billing model for `<account>`?" and "what is `<account>`'s 2026 service fee?" in one hop, at operator scope. **Rates are NOT here** - they live in REF-141 (the generated Price Book), which is the ONLY canonical price-values document. That delegation is deliberate: a second rate surface is a second staleness surface.

**How this file is kept honest.** Shapes come from REF-140 §Per-topic model (c). Headline figures come from REF-141 per-account headers and are cross-checked against PG `sc_fee_schedule` on every regeneration. Any figure discrepancy stops the batch that regenerates this doc.

## The four billing shapes (from REF-140 §c)

| Shape | Meaning | Which accounts |
|---|---|---|
| **SF%** | Per-meal + Service Fee that DISCOUNTS the per-meal rate (the SF% is baked into the invoice rate). | CIN-AZ (30%), TXR-AZ (20% via deposit-triggered discount), TBR-FL MiLB (25% via buy-down) |
| **Flat-SF** | Per-meal + Service Fee that runs INDEPENDENT of the per-meal invoice (SF billed on its own schedule; per-meal at full sticker). | TBJ-FL |
| **No-SF** | Pure per-meal, no separate service fee. | CIN-KY, TBJ-NY, TBR-FL MLB, BGC (second-client on TBR-FL, prepaid 4-week periods) |
| **Flat_fee** | Contracted flat fee is the money. Per-meal counts exist in SC for planning only; the fee lives in `sc_fee_schedule`. | CIN-OH, STL-MO, STL-FL, TXR-TX-H, TXR-TX-V |

## Four special cases

**STL-FL is billing-model flat_fee but chefs enter meal counts.** From REF-140 §g: "STL-FL is billing-model `flat_fee` but operationally a PDC (phases, camps, MiLB affiliates). Chefs enter meal counts for planning. The tile SHOULD show meals, not dollars - the calendar's `isFeeAccount && hasHomestandMap` gate correctly falls through to per-meal display (`fee-no-dollar` variant) because STL-FL has no homestand schedule (it's a PDC, not an MLB). Chefs get the operational surface they need; the $1.4M fee lives in `sc_fee_schedule` for the KPI dashboard." The count entry is for planning / ordering / labor / waste; not for billing.

**TXR-TX-V is $0 covered by H's carve-in.** The `sc_fee_schedule` entry for TXR-TX-V is amount=0 with `covered_by_account_key = "TXR - TX - H"`. This covers the visiting-clubhouse G&G / snacks / coffee that TXR-TX-H's $604,032 contract carve-in includes. **The real visiting-team direct-sales catering revenue is a separate stream tracked in the Season Tracker workflow** (sold-through revenue x 19.23% labor model), out of scope for both SC and the fee schedule. When someone asks "does the visiting clubhouse make money for KitchFix?" the answer is "yes, via direct-sales catering in Season Tracker; no separate fee schedule entry."

**BGC is contractually independent from TBR-FL.** REF-121 §D: "BGC is fully independent contractually; the commissary-model overlap is operational, not contractual." Billing entity = "Boys & Girls Clubs of Charlotte County" at 21500 Gibralter Drive, Port Charlotte, FL - a distinct entity from the Tampa Bay Rays. **Term ends May 21, 2026 (no auto-renew).** Half of the 2026 calendar year has no BGC service until a school-year renewal lands. **Payment method: Check only** (§VII; ACH and CC are unchecked in the contract).

**TBR-FL is two billing entities in one operation.** MLB (per-meal, NO SF) and MiLB (per-meal + 25% buy-down via SF) bill to SEPARATE client cost centers (Erik Hart for MLB, Sean "Sunny" Jones for MiLB). One commissary, one operation, two invoice streams by design. Plus BGC as a THIRD billing entity on the same commissary.

## Postseason mechanics (flat_fee accounts only)

- **CIN-OH postseason:** 1/81-of-fee per game = **$4,413.58/game** if the Reds qualify. Same escalation as the regular-season fee.
- **STL-MO postseason:** flat per-game (**Game $5,222.22 / Workout $2,777.78 / Road Food $600**), NOT escalated. Differs from CIN-OH which escalates its postseason rate alongside its regular season.
- **TXR-TX-H:** no postseason mentioned as escalating (annual negotiation).

## Service-fee tax treatment

- **SF tax-TAXABLE:** CIN-OH 7.80%, TXR-TX-H 8.25% (Arlington TX gross-up)
- **SF tax-EXEMPT:** STL-FL (invoice-confirmed)
- **SF NON-TAXABLE:** STL-MO (Cardinals' legal position, invoice-confirmed K300168469)
- **Ancillary tax-free lines:** CIN-AZ Coffee + Fountain Bev (weekly flat); TBR-FL BGC Lunch ($6.50, after-school supper program)

## Where details live - precedence

- **Rates (per-meal $, unit-flat $):** REF-141 Price Book. Regenerated from PG on any price change. This is the ONLY canonical price-values document.
- **Fee amounts (2026 headline figure):** REF-141 header line per account; PG `sc_fee_schedule` for the flat_fee subset. Both cohere at load time.
- **Contract source-of-record:** REF-121 through REF-132 (Contract Digests). These are historical + verbatim; supersede annotations name when a stale contract number has been overtaken by finance-confirmed figures (see TBJ-FL for the pattern - REF-127's $452,812 vs finance $515,712).
- **Per-account rulings + operational context + stakeholder identity:** REC-101 through REC-111 (Account Records; restricted).
- **Billing shape taxonomy definitions + why:** REF-140 §Per-topic model (c) + (d) + (g).
- **This quick reference:** REF-142 (this file) - operator-scope, unrestricted, no per-meal rates.

If a figure here disagrees with REF-141 or PG, **REF-141 and PG win** and this file gets regenerated.
