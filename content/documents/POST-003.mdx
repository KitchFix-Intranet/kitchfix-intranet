# Kitchen Safety - Posted Summary

A wall-mounted reference for every kitchen. The binding text is SOP-008 Food Safety Management. This posting is the floor-facing summary built under STD-002 v0.2 visual standard.

## Eight focus areas (icon tiles)

- **Knives** - keep them sharp, walk with the blade down and back to your body, never catch a falling knife.
- **Hot surfaces** - "behind, hot, knife, heavy" call-out is the kitchen language; PPE per task per PB-007.
- **Slip / trip / fall** - clean as you go, dry the floor, never run, mark wet floors.
- **Burns and fire** - cover burns immediately, grease fires get smothered not watered, know where the fire extinguisher and pull station are.
- **Chemicals and HazCom** - SDS sheets accessible per OSHA HazCom; never mix; quat sanitizer <Fact id="quat_ppm" /> per SOP-008 §10.
- **Lifting and ergonomics** - lift with legs, get help on anything over 50 lb, use the cart.
- **Allergens** - the Top 9 per PB-002; cross-contact prevention disciplines per PB-002 §03.
- **Hygiene and health** - handwashing per SOP-008 §11; sick staff do not handle food per FORM-008.

## In an emergency

- Call 911 first if life-safety risk exists.
- Stabilize and remove the hazard.
- Notify per SOP-002 severity matrix (see POST-001 for the call-by-tier reference).
- Document in the People Portal Incident Center.

## Bilingual

This posting ships as one bilingual sheet (EN + ES combined). Per STD-002 §08, safety content is strongly recommended for bilingual production. Translation workflow per STD-002 §8.3.

## Where to find the binding standard

The full food-safety management system - all 15 sections, every threshold, every monitoring requirement - lives in SOP-008 Food Safety Management. Related kitchen-safety detail (knives, PPE, ergonomics, HazCom, fire) lives in PB-007 Workplace Safety Manual.
