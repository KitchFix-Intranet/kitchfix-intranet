# The Big Rules - Posted Summary

A wall-mounted reference for every KitchFix PFS employee. The binding text is AGR-001 The Big Rules. This posting is the at-a-glance reminder of the nine rules and the confidentiality anchor.

## Confidentiality - the anchor

Everything in the clubhouse is confidential. If you learned it because you were in the Club, treat it as confidential. There is no inner circle exception. Confidentiality does not expire.

## The Nine Clubhouse Rules

1. **No pictures or video recording** of players, coaches, Club staff, or Club facilities while on Club premises.
2. **No autographs** - do not ask players, coaches, or Club staff for autographs.
3. **No talk of gambling** - do not bet, do not discuss betting lines or fantasy outcomes with Club personnel.
4. **No talk of steroid use** - do not discuss, speculate about, or joke about player PED use.
5. **No talking to media or reporters** - refer them to the Club's communications team or to KitchFix leadership.
6. **No inside information** - trades, lineups, injuries, contracts, travel - not yours to share.
7. **No dietary advice** - we are not dietitians; route nutritional questions to the team dietitian.
8. **No fraternizing** with players, coaches, or Club staff.
9. **No soliciting** - do not offer services, products, or businesses to Club personnel.

## Escalation

All KitchFix-internal work issues go to KitchFix leadership first. You do not raise KitchFix issues with the Client. Ever.

## Where to find the full rules

The binding text and full MLR 21 quotation live in AGR-001 The Big Rules - signed by every PFS employee at hire.
