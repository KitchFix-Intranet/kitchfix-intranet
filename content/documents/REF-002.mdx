# Purpose & Scope

This catalog is the operational reference for all KitchFix uniform items. It governs vendor, color, SKU, and procurement for every item in the uniform system.

This document is a companion to POL-002 Appearance & Dress Code Policy, which governs the rules for wearing and maintaining the uniform. This catalog governs procurement. When a vendor, SKU, price, or process changes, this document is updated - not the Policy.

> NOTE: Prices shown are estimates as of the effective date. Verify current pricing at time of order. Site-specific linen costs vary by account and linen rotation agreement.

# Ordering & Procurement Notes

Managers are responsible for ordering, tracking, and processing uniform items for their account. Use the People Portal on the intranet to submit PAFs for any payroll deductions.

Custom Ink (KitchFix T-Shirt): orders are placed by site managers through Ops. Use the shared Custom Ink design link - do not create a new design.

Chef Works (Polo / Chef Coats): orders placed online at chefworks.com using the KitchFix account login. Embroidery spec must be followed exactly per the catalog above.

Shoes for Crews: employees can purchase directly using the corporate discount, or managers may purchase and deduct via PAF. The employee must meet the non-slip black shoe specification regardless of source.

Linen items (aprons, kitchen coats, prep shirts): coordinated through the local linen company and linen rotation agreement for each site. Contact Ops for account-specific linen vendor information.

> NOTE: PAF = Personnel Action Form. Submit via the People Portal on the intranet for any payroll withholding or deduction related to uniform procurement.
