# How to Use This Template

Read this first. It explains the ten-section structure, the floor-then-override hierarchy, and the three things you will see on every page.

> ANCHOR: This template is the shell. KitchFix Level standards are pre-stated as the floor. Your job is to confirm the floor, tune what is account-specific, and document any client override. The floor holds at every account unless an override is written down and signed off.

## The Ten-Section Structure

Every account SLA follows the same ten sections, in the same order, so a leader who knows where Section 4 lives in one SLA knows where it lives in every other one. Account-specific content fills the structure; the structure does not change.

Section 1 - Commercial Terms - reviewed by legal before execution. Updated at renewal.

Sections 2-10 - Operating SLA - reviewed annually at the ABR. Material changes mid-year are documented as amendments and folded in at the next ABR.

## The Floor-Then-Override Hierarchy

KitchFix Level standards apply at every account by default. The Allergen Playbook (PB-002), the Culinary OS floor (PB-006), the Big Rules (AGR-001), ServSafe currency, and the menu approval workflow are the floor. Where the client's own guidelines are silent, the KitchFix floor applies.

Account-level overrides are documented in the section the override applies to - with the rationale and the signing authority who approved it. KitchFix Level SOPs are never edited to accommodate an account. The override lives here, in the SLA.

## Three Things You Will See on Every Page

Floor - A line led by the bold navy word "Floor" states a KitchFix Level standard. Carry it forward. Only change it by documenting an override.

Write-in fields - Italic gray text in brackets, like [ fill in ], is a prompt. Replace it with the account-specific answer, or write "N/A-" with a reason. Never leave it bracketed in a finished draft.

OPEN notes - A gray NOTE callout that begins "OPEN-" is a question or gap for the site team to close. These are expected in a working draft. Resolve each one or escalate it before the draft goes to the client.

> NOTE: This is a working template, not a finished SLA. Blanks, bracketed prompts, and OPEN notes are the point - they tell the site team exactly what to fill and what to confirm. Polish comes later, on the way to the client conversation.

Floor - Every menu cycle passes the same workflow before service: Site Leader builds the menu - Director of Culinary reviews - Site Leader adjusts from feedback and submits to client - client gives feedback and/or approves. Menu changes require SLA + Director of Culinary + Dietitian + CHM (where applicable) sign-off.

Floor - Latin every meal period is a KitchFix non-negotiable culinary commitment.

### Cycle structure & lead time

Floor - PDC - four-to-six-week rotating cycle, refreshed seasonally (Spring Training, Extended ST, FCL, Instructional League). Two-week menu lead.

Floor - MLB Clubhouse - series-based cycle; one approved menu document per home series, one tab per game day named by date. Seven-day lead, forty-eight-hour final lock.

Floor - Visiting clubhouses - series-based menus per the client's catering requests.

Floor - Substitutions for product quality or availability are documented same-day and communicated to the dietitian.

# 05 Nutrition Guidelines and Allergen Standards

Nutrition philosophy and guidelines, portion standards, allergen handling, and emergency response. The Allergen Playbook (PB-002) is the company-wide standard; account overrides are documented here.

## Nutrition Guidelines & Portion Standards

## Protein Framework (floor)

Lean - Primary direction at every meal period - skinless chicken breast, turkey, white-fleshed fish, lean beef (flank, ground, tres major, sirloin), pork tenderloin. Lean leads the menu.

Heavy - Reserved primarily for arrival or dinner - chicken thighs, brisket, short rib, sausages, pork shoulder.

High-end - Per budget and client requirements, traditionally post-game - beef tenderloin, ribeye, striploin, sea bass, halibut.

Cadence - Lean every meal period. Cured/processed meats cadence controlled per SLA. Heavy and high-end per budget and SLA.

## Ingredient Principles (floor)

Grains & starches - Full carbohydrate library. Breads whole-grain through gluten-free. Rice white through brown, basmati, jasmine, sushi, arborio. Pasta regular, whole grain, couscous, chickpea, gluten-free. Potatoes, sweet potatoes, plantains, yuca, and other tubers carry the same weight as grains. The cuisine and meal period decide which carbohydrate is on the line.

Dairy - Whole, skim, chocolate, and non-dairy alternatives per SLA. Greek yogurt, cream cheese, sour cream, half-and-half, non-dairy creamers, butter. Fresh, aged, and culturally specific cheeses chosen by the dish. Avoid highly processed cheeses (American slices, jar sauces, pre-melted blends) unless permitted.

Vegetables & fruit - Variety is the operating principle - across categories and preparations so the same vegetable rarely lands the same way twice in a week. Fruit covers tropical, stone, citrus, berry, melon - whole and fresh-cut, focused on seasonality. Pre-game skews away from cruciferous and chili-heavy preparations for digestive comfort.

Approved fats & oils - Avocado oil (high-heat), extra virgin olive oil (finishing/salad/low-heat), olive oil (medium-heat), sesame oil (Asian finishing), butter (sparingly), beef tallow (par-frying only when SLA approves), specialty fats* (truffle oil, coconut oil, ghee). *Allergens must always be identified.

## Restricted and Prohibited Products (floor)

### Restricted - clarify in SLA before use

Pre-breaded items (freshly breaded and baked permitted per SLA) · frozen prepared items as a primary protein/starch/vegetable · iceberg lettuce (approved greens: spinach, chopped romaine, kale, arugula, spring mix) · highly processed snacks on the open line · peanuts/tree nuts as individually contained, labeled toppings or single-use packaging · soy sauce as a retail bottle on the sauce station or in pre-made sauces with transparent allergen labeling.

### Prohibited - never used in our operations

Seed oils (grapeseed, canola, sunflower, soy, blends) · nut oils (peanut, tree nut) · peanuts/tree nuts as a buffet-line ingredient · soy sauce as a buffet production ingredient (use tamari or coconut aminos).

## Cooking Methods (floor)

Approved - Grilling, baking, roasting, broiling, pan-searing, sautéing, steaming, braising/stewing, poaching. Par-frying with approved oils, baked to finish, only when approved in the SLA.

Prohibited - Deep frying. Microwaves may be provided to the club for players to reheat their own food only.

> CRITICAL: Par-frying is permitted only when the SLA approves it, with approved oils, baked to finish. Deep frying is prohibited at every account.

## Allergen Position

> CRITICAL: The KitchFix Allergen Playbook (PB-002) applies in full at every account and is the single source of truth. No allergen-free meal or station claims - ever. Severe allergy is a Chef-level case-by-case decision in coordination with the team dietitian.

### Standing positions (floor)

Top 9 allergens (milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans, sesame) tracked and labeled at every meal period in English and Spanish; labeling complete before food enters the line.

No peanuts or tree nuts on the open buffet; limited individually-packaged exceptions with clear labeling per the Playbook.

Vendor substitutions evaluated against the original allergen profile before service; new Top 9 allergens require dietitian notification.

Segregated storage, dedicated utensils, color-coded handling per the Playbook.

No account-level overrides unless documented here with rationale, approval trail, and Director of Culinary sign-off.

## Severe Allergy and Emergency Response

Floor - Players, coaches, or staff with severe allergies are flagged to KitchFix in advance by the team dietitian. Accommodation plans are built per individual through the Chef and dietitian. Anaphylaxis - team medical staff is the primary responder; KitchFix Site Leader is notified immediately, food prep in the affected area is paused, 911 is contacted by team medical. KitchFix preserves food samples, ingredient lists, and prep records as requested.
