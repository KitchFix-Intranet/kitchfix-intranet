# 01 Purpose and Scope

Keeping KitchFix drivers, the public, and our cargo safe on the road.

This SOP governs anyone who operates a KitchFix or business-use vehicle - delivery vans, box trucks, and personal vehicles used for KitchFix business. It covers who may drive, how vehicles are operated and inspected, and what happens after an accident. Accident reporting itself follows SOP-002 §07.2.

# 02 Driver Eligibility

- **Valid license** - a current, valid driver's license for the vehicle class, on file with People Operations.
- **Driving record** - an acceptable motor-vehicle record, checked before authorization and periodically after.
- **Authorized drivers only** - only HR-authorized employees operate KitchFix vehicles. No exceptions for a quick run.
- **No impairment** - never drive under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or any medication that impairs. This ties to POL-003.
- **Report changes** - a license suspension, DUI, or major violation is reported to HR immediately.

# 03 Vehicle Use and Daily Inspection

Before the first trip of the day, the driver walks the vehicle:

- Tires, lights, mirrors, brakes, horn, and wipers working. Fluids and fuel adequate.
- Interior clean; cargo area clean and food-safe for any transported food.
- Load secured so it cannot shift, fall, or block the driver.
- Refrigerated or hot-held food is transported per SOP-008 - the cold or hot chain does not break in transit.

> NOTE: If a daily inspection turns up an unsafe condition - bald tire, bad brakes, broken light - the vehicle is tagged out and not driven until repaired. See §06.

# 04 Safe Driving Rules

- Seatbelts on - driver and every passenger, every trip.
- No handheld phone use. Calls and navigation are set before driving or hands-free.
- Obey speed limits and traffic law. Slow for weather, traffic, and lots.
- Park legally and safely. Set the brake. Never leave the vehicle running and unattended.
- Back up only when necessary - use a spotter in tight lots and at loading docks.
- Plan the route and time. Fatigue and rush cause crashes.

# 05 Accident Response

Any injury makes this an S1 - call 911 first. Then work the steps.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 - Stop and secure | Stop safely. Turn on hazards. Check everyone for injury. |
| 2 - Call 911 | Any injury - call 911 immediately. This is an S1 incident. |
| 3 - Call police | For any collision, injury, or property damage. Get the police report number. |
| 4 - Exchange and document | Exchange insurance and contact info. Photograph all vehicles, plates, and the scene. Do not admit fault or discuss cause. |
| 5 - Notify | Notify People Operations and the VP of Operations - the VPO is notified for all vehicle incidents regardless of severity. |
| 6 - Report | File the incident in the People Portal per SOP-002 §07.2 within the SLA for the tier. |

> CRITICAL: Never admit fault, speculate on cause, or offer payment at the scene. Document the facts, exchange information, and let the process work.

# 06 Maintenance and Records

- Vehicles follow a scheduled maintenance plan - oil, tires, brakes, inspection.
- Drivers report mechanical issues the day they appear. Unsafe vehicles are tagged out until repaired.
- Maintenance, mileage, and inspection records are kept for each vehicle.
- Registration, insurance, and any required commercial permits stay current. Permits and licenses are governed by POL-019.
