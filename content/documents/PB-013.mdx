# Purpose & How to Use

How KitchFix trains and certifies its people - so everyone on the line is ready for the job they're doing.

> ANCHOR: A site is only as good as its weakest-trained person on the line. Training isn't a one-time event at hire - it's the system that keeps a seasonal, scaling workforce at one standard.

This program defines how KitchFix trains, develops, and certifies its workforce - from onboarding through role mastery, required certifications, and leadership development. It applies to every site and every role, and it works with onboarding (SOP-005), the onboarding checklist (TPL-016), and the systems that track it all.

> NOTE: [OPEN - HR INPUT] The specific certification matrix - which certifications are required by role and by state, and the providers used - is finalized with HR and reflected in Section 05. This program sets the structure; the matrix is the detail.

# The Training Philosophy

Train before you deploy. Competence, not time served.

- Verified, not assumed - nobody runs a station solo until they're trained and, where required, verified for it (SOP-005, FORM-009).
- Consistent everywhere - the same standard at every site - a cook trained at one account meets the same bar at another.
- Built for the season - a workforce that swings 50 to 300 needs training that scales - fast onboarding, then depth over the season.
- Grow your people - rehiring proven, developed staff season to season is how a site keeps its standard without starting over each spring.

> ANCHOR: The goal isn't to have trained someone once. It's that any guest, any shift, any site, gets the same standard - because the people behind it were built to deliver it.

# Onboarding Training

Every new hire's first training - the foundation everything else builds on.

Onboarding training is delivered through SOP-005 and tracked on the onboarding checklist (TPL-016). Before a new hire works a station alone, they complete the foundation:

- The Big Rules and core policies (AGR-001) - conduct, confidentiality, the non-negotiables.
- Workplace safety basics and incident reporting (PB-007, SOP-002).
- Food-safety fundamentals and allergen awareness (SOP-008, PB-002).
- Knife-skills verification before knife-station work (FORM-009).

> NOTE: Onboarding training is the floor, not the finish. It makes a new hire safe and compliant; role training (Section 04) makes them good.

# Role-Based Training

Each role trained to its own standard - a cook, an attendant, and a dishwasher need different things.

| Role | Training focus |
|---|---|
| Cook | Stations, recipes and production, knife skills, food safety, the line. |
| FOH Attendant | Service, hospitality, presentation, allergen awareness, the guest experience. |
| Dishwasher | Warewashing, sanitation, the cleaning schedule, safe equipment use. |
| Leadership triad | Everything above, plus people, P&L, client, and the systems that run a site (PB-010). |

Role training is delivered on site by the Executive Chef and leadership triad, to the standards in the operating manuals, and recorded as complete before the person works the role independently.

# Required Certifications

The certifications the law and the standard require - held, current, and on file.

- Food safety certification - food-handler and/or manager food-safety certification per role and per state requirement.
- Allergen awareness - allergen training aligned to the Allergen Playbook (PB-002).
- Knife-skills verification - competency verification (FORM-009) - internal, not a license, but required before knife-station work.
- State / local requirements - any certification a specific state or jurisdiction requires for the role.

> NOTE: [OPEN - HR INPUT] The required-certification matrix - by role, by state, with providers and renewal periods - is built with HR. KitchFix operates in eight states (AZ, FL, IL, KY, MO, NY, OH, TX); requirements differ by jurisdiction and must be mapped, not assumed.

# Food Safety & Allergen Training

The training that protects the guest - the one area with no shortcuts.

Food safety and allergen training is foundational and ongoing, not a one-time hurdle. It is delivered to the standards that govern the kitchen, and it points to the documents that own those standards:

- Food safety - cook, hold, cool, store, hygiene, and cross-contamination - to SOP-008.
- Allergens - the Top 9, no allergen-free claims, severe-allergy accommodation handled correctly - to PB-002.
- Banned substances - for player-facing product, the sourcing and verification standard (SOP-009).
- Refreshed - reinforced regularly and at inspection-readiness checks (CHK-003) - not left to fade after onboarding.

> CRITICAL: Food-safety competence is verified, not presumed. An employee who can't demonstrate safe handling isn't ready for the line, however long they've worked.

# Leadership Development

Growing the people who run sites - the bench that lets KitchFix scale.

Leadership development builds the triad and the RDO bench - the people who carry the standard when the company grows. It runs through the Leadership Dugout: the performance systems and development tools that coach leaders cycle over cycle.

- Leadership performance system - structured development and review for site leaders (SOP-001).
- Hourly performance system - development for the hourly team - the pipeline into leadership (SOP-007).
- Scorecards & reviews - the cadence tools that make development concrete (TPL-001/002/003/004/010).

> NOTE: The Leadership Dugout build set is staged for August. This section names where leadership development lives; those tools carry the detail as they come online.

# Certification Tracking & Compliance

Every certification tracked, current, and provable - in one system.

- Tracked in Rippling - certifications and training completion are recorded and tracked in Rippling - the single source for who is certified for what.
- Renewals managed - expiration dates are tracked and renewals scheduled before they lapse - a lapsed required certification is a compliance gap.
- Verifiable - at any time, a site should be able to show its team's certifications are current - for a client, an inspector, or an audit.

> ANCHOR: "We think they're certified" is not a record. If it isn't in Rippling and current, it doesn't count when it matters.

# Training Records

If it isn't recorded, it didn't happen.

Training and verification are documented as they're completed and kept in the employee record. The record is what proves the standard was met - for compliance, for the client, and for the next manager who needs to know what this person is trained for.

- Onboarding and role training recorded against the checklist (TPL-016) and in Rippling.
- Certifications and verifications (including FORM-009) filed in the employee record.
- Records retained per the company's retention practice.

# Roles & Responsibilities

Clear ownership so training and certification never lapse between roles.

- People Operations - owns the program - the certification matrix, tracking in Rippling, and compliance.
- Executive Chef / leadership triad - delivers onboarding and role training on site and verifies competence before deployment.
- Director of Culinary - owns culinary and food-safety training standards and the banned-substance program (SOP-009).
- RDO - ensures sites train consistently and develops the leadership bench across the region.
- The employee - completes required training and keeps their certifications current.
