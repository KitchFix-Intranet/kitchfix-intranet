# 01 Purpose and Scope

Coverage is the product. A clubhouse meal that lands late is a service failure the client sees immediately, and an uncovered shift falls on the people who did show up. This Policy sets the attendance and punctuality standard that keeps every site staffed and every meal on time.

> ANCHOR: We staff for the meal period or the homestand before the meal or the homestand. Reliable attendance is not a courtesy to your team - it is how we keep the promise of <Fact id="brand_promise" /> on a schedule we do not control.

This Policy applies to every KitchFix employee, regardless of role, employment type, or account. The scheduling, clock-in, and call-out rules apply to anyone who works a scheduled shift, and leadership is held to the same standard - and models it first.

Attendance is part of performance. Punctuality, reliability, and proper notice are evaluated like any other part of the job, and patterns are addressed through SOP-004 Formal Disciplinary Process, up to and including termination.

> NOTE: This Policy is not a contract of employment and does not alter the at-will employment relationship.

# 02 Definitions

The terms below carry specific meaning throughout this Policy.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Scheduled Shift | A shift assigned to you in Rippling, including its start time, end time, and site. |
| On Time | Clocked in, in uniform, and ready to work at your station at your scheduled start time - not merely arriving at the building. |
| Tardy | Being ready to work after your scheduled start time. |
| Absence | Missing a scheduled shift, in whole or in part. |
| Call-Out | Notifying your Site Leader that you will be absent or late for a scheduled shift. |
| Excused Absence | An absence approved in advance, protected by law, or covered by approved time off (see Section 07). |
| Unexcused Absence | An absence that is not approved, not protected, and not reported under the call-out procedure. |
| No-Call/No-Show | Missing a scheduled shift without notifying your Site Leader. |
| Game Day / Peak Period | A game day and its service windows, plus homestands, Spring Training, and the Florida Complex League. |
| Protected Leave | Time off protected by federal, state, or local law (see Section 07). |

# 03 Scheduling and Shift Expectations

Coverage starts with a published schedule and the expectation that everyone is ready to work when their shift begins.

## The Schedule

- Schedules are published in Rippling at least 7 days in advance. Check Rippling for your shifts, start times, and site.
- If you see a coverage gap or a conflict, raise it with your Site Leader as early as possible. Coverage gaps are filled before service, not during it.

## Being On Time

- On time means ready to work - clocked in, in uniform, at your station - at your scheduled start time, not just arriving at the building.
- Hourly employees may not clock in more than 5 minutes before their scheduled start time, and must clock out promptly at the end of the shift. Working off the clock is not permitted.

## Shift Trades and Coverage

- A shift trade or coverage change must be approved by your Site Leader in advance and reflected in Rippling.
- Until your Site Leader confirms the coverage, the shift is still yours. Finding a coworker to swap is not the same as an approved trade.

# 04 Reporting an Absence or Lateness

When you cannot make a shift, the standard is simple: tell your Site Leader, directly, as early as you can.

## Planned Absences

Request time off in advance through the time-off process in Rippling. Planned absences are not call-outs - submit them as early as possible so coverage can be built into the schedule.

## Unplanned Absences and Lateness (Call-Outs)

- As soon as you know - notify your Site Leader the moment you know you will be absent or late - and no later than 2 hours before your scheduled start time.
- How - call or text your Site Leader directly, and log the absence in Rippling. A text to a coworker is not a call-out.
- If you cannot reach your Site Leader - contact your Sous Chef or Hospitality Manager, then your Regional Director. Reach a leader - never leave it to a coworker, and never involve the client.
- Each day - for a multi-day absence, check in each day unless your Site Leader has set a different arrangement.

> NOTE: Genuine emergencies and sudden illness are judged on what was reasonable - notify us as soon as you are able. The 2-hour expectation is a coverage standard, not a penalty for a true emergency.

# 05 Game-Day and Peak-Period Expectations

Game days, homestands, Spring Training, and the FCL/ACL run hot. Coverage is hardest to find and the stakes are highest, because the meal still has to hit the service window.

> ANCHOR: We are proactive, not reactive. We staff for the homestand before the homestand - and on a game day, every person is part of a plan that cannot easily absorb a surprise.

The attendance standard does not change on a game day. In practice, it tightens.

- Give all the notice you can - a 2-hour call-out on a game day may not leave enough time to cover the shift. If you know the night before, or the morning of, reach out then - not at the 2-hour mark.
- No game-day trades without approval - shift trades and coverage changes on game days, homestands, or during Spring Training require Site Leader approval and a confirmed replacement before the change is real.

# 06 Attendance Accountability

Patterns of lateness, unexcused absence, or short-notice call-outs are addressed like any other performance issue - coached first, then through SOP-004 if the pattern continues.

KitchFix does not run a points or occurrence system. Attendance concerns are weighed in context - how often, the pattern over time, how much notice you gave, and the impact on service - and the same situation is handled the same way for everyone.

## How Patterns Are Handled

- Attendance is tracked in Rippling. Your Site Leader sees the record and coaches in the moment, privately, before anything is written up.
- A continuing pattern moves into SOP-004 Formal Disciplinary Process - documented and progressive, up to and including termination, at KitchFix's discretion.

## No-Call/No-Show

- One no-call/no-show - missing a scheduled shift without notifying your Site Leader is a serious violation on its own and is addressed under SOP-004.
- Two consecutive no-call/no-shows - two consecutive scheduled shifts missed with no call are treated as voluntary resignation - you will be considered to have abandoned the position.

> NOTE: Absences that are approved or protected by law are never counted against you here. See Section 07.

# 07 Protected and Approved Absences

Some absences are protected by law or approved in advance. These are never counted against your attendance and are never disciplined.

Approved time off and absences protected by federal, state, or local law are excused. They do not factor into the accountability process in Section 06.

## Protected Categories

Protected leave includes, but is not limited to:

- Family and medical leave (FMLA).
- Reasonable accommodation for a disability or pregnancy (ADA and related law).
- Paid sick leave required by state or local law.
- Jury duty and legally required court appearances.
- Voting leave.
- Military service and related leave (USERRA).
- Workers' compensation, and any other leave protected by applicable law.

## How to Use It

- Route it through People Operations - if you need leave for a protected reason or an accommodation, contact People Operations. Give as much notice as the situation allows; for sudden needs, notify us as soon as practicable.
- Managers do not decide protected leave - Site Leaders and managers do not approve, deny, or judge protected-leave or accommodation requests - those go to People Operations.

> CRITICAL: Never discipline an employee for a protected or approved absence. If you are unsure whether an absence is protected, do not act - contact People Operations first.

# 08 Manager Responsibilities

Site Leaders own coverage. Consistent, documented, fair handling of attendance is what keeps a team staffed and keeps KitchFix protected.

## Run the Schedule

- Publish the schedule in Rippling at least 7 days out, and build coverage plans for homestands, Spring Training, and known peak periods before they arrive.
- Keep punches and exceptions accurate in Rippling; clear corrections before payroll close.

## Handle Attendance

- Coach attendance in the moment and in private. Recognize reliability; address slippage early.
- Document patterns and escalate them to your Regional Director and People Operations through SOP-004. Do not let a pattern run uncoached.
- Apply it consistently - treat the same situation the same way for every employee. Consistency is both the fair thing and the legal protection - uneven enforcement is what creates risk.

## Protected Leave

Route any protected-leave or accommodation question to People Operations. Do not deny, delay, or discipline a protected absence.

> NOTE: When attendance and a possible protected reason overlap, stop and call People Operations before taking any action.
