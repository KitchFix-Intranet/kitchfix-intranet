# 01 Purpose and Scope

STD-002 governs the visual artifacts KitchFix produces - postings, posters, infographics, and signage. Different register from body docs. Same brand discipline.

STD-001 governs body documents - playbooks, standards, policies, SOPs. STD-002 governs visual artifacts that operate on a different register: large-format, scannable from across a room, often laminated, often replaced quarterly.

> ANCHOR: A visual artifact must do its job in three seconds at six feet. Body docs earn the reader's attention because the reader sat down to read them. Visuals do not get that grace.

## In Scope

- Postings (POST) - one-page operator references intended for posting in kitchens, line-of-sight from work stations.
- Posters (POSTER) - large-format brand and culture artifacts intended for break rooms, common areas, hallways.
- Infographics (INFO) - one-page or one-slide explainers that communicate a system, a process, or a decision tree.
- Signage (SIGN) - wayfinding, station labels, equipment labels, and hazard markers.

## Out of Scope

- Body documents - governed by STD-001.
- Memos, emails, Slack - governed by STD-003 (pending).
- External-facing collateral - governed by external brand standards.
- Menu boards and menu cards - governed by Culinary, not Operations.

# 02 Visual Document Classes

Four classes. POST, POSTER, INFO, SIGN. Each has a defined purpose, document ID prefix, and expected lifecycle.

Source documents - every visual artifact derives from a source document. POST derives from SOP/AGR/POL; POSTER from PB/AGR/values; INFO from PB/SOP; SIGN from operational standard.

> CRITICAL: Visuals never replace the source document. They reference it. A POST that contradicts its source SOP is non-compliant by definition.

# 03 Format Specifications

- POST default 8.5 by 11 inch portrait, US Letter. Office-printer compatible. Tabloid 11 by 17 alternate for high-traffic.
- POSTER 18 by 24 inch portrait standard; 24 by 36 alternate for high-visibility locations.
- INFO 8.5 by 11 portrait OR 16:9 slide ratio.
- SIGN variable by use case.

> CRITICAL: A POST that cannot print on a standard office color printer is non-compliant.

# 04 Typography for Visuals

14pt body minimum on POST and POSTER. Tile-internal exception (8.5pt on POST/INFO, 10pt on POSTER) when the tile carries an icon plus a hero-scale name and the small text is reference-list data, not the primary safety message.

Fonts: Oswald Bold for Hero and Section Header; Mulish Bold for Subhead, Mulish Regular for Body. Arial fallback chain identical to STD-001.

# 05 Color System for Visuals

Brand colors from STD-001 plus the severity color system. Severity tiers map to fills and tints:

- S1 Critical Red #B53030, Red Tint #FCE4E4. Reserved for life safety.
- S2 Caution Amber #D87E1E, Amber Tint #FBEFD0. Hybrid treatment: Amber Tint fill with Critical Red border 1.5pt to signal medical-attention urgency without using full Critical Red.
- S3 Standard Green #1F7A3D, Green Tint #DFF0E2. First-aid-resolved incidents.
- S4 Standard Green, Green Tint, distinguished from S3 by icon.

> ANCHOR: Color is never the only signal. Every severity color pairs with a text label and an icon. The triple-signal pattern is non-negotiable.

# 06 Iconography

Iconify-aggregated icon library. Lucide preferred, MDI second, Tabler third, Phosphor fourth, Font Awesome fifth, Game Icons for food/ingredient icons.

Severity icon mapping locked: S1 = Lucide alert-triangle filled red. S2 = Lucide alert-circle outlined amber. S3 = Lucide info outlined green. S4 = Lucide check-circle outlined green.

# 07 Layout Patterns

## 7.1 Three-Zone POST Layout

Hero (top 15-20%), Content (middle 65-70%), Footer (bottom 10-15%).

> ANCHOR: Two-to-four content blocks. Never more. A POST with five or more content blocks is two POSTs disguised as one.

## 7.2 Single-Focus POSTER Layout

A poster makes one statement. Focal element 50-70% of visual weight.

## 7.5 During / After Time Separation

Multi-step processes spanning during-event and after-event time horizons get visually separated.

## 7.6 Reactive Above Reflective

In-the-moment instructions sit above between-event reference content. Allergen Reaction Response panel sits above the 9-allergen reference grid; Severity tier table sits above the 6-step process.

## 7.7 Phone-Tree Gap Pattern

When a POST carries a phone-tree contact list, an explicit instruction band names what to do when the primary contact does not answer. Phone numbers do NOT appear on wall artifacts; directory lookups only.

> CRITICAL: Phone numbers belong in the directory, not on the wall. Posting personal cell numbers in clubhouses, kitchens, or break rooms exposes them to anyone in the building.

# 08 Bilingual Standards EN+ES

EN+ES is recommended, not required. Safety, allergen, and incident-response content strongly recommended for ES. Translation workflow: AI drafts, Sr Director Operations reviews, People Operations approves, both languages publish together. EN and ES share version numbers.

# 09 Logo Use on Visuals

STD-001 §6 governs the logo system. Visual-specific exceptions: smaller minimum sizes (0.4 inch tall on POST), alpha-channel verification required on master logo files (raw KitchFix-supplied PNGs are RGB with black backplates baked in; strip before use, save with "_t" suffix).

# 10 Production and Print Specs

Office printer for POST and INFO portrait (24lb white paper, matte, sRGB, no bleed). Print shop for POSTER and large-format SIGN.

# 11 Editable Source Files

Every visual artifact ships as a pair: an editable PPTX master in `/Visual Artifacts/Sources/` and a published PDF in `/Visual Artifacts/Published/`. PPTX is the master; PDF is the artifact. Edits happen in the PPTX.

> ANCHOR: PPTX is the master. PDF is the artifact. Edits happen in the PPTX. The PDF is regenerated from the PPTX at every version cut.

# 12 Lifecycle and Update Cadence

POST quarterly review, POSTER annual, INFO at source SOP review, SIGN annual or per-event. Corner version stamp on every artifact (bottom-right).

# 13 Approval and Review Chain

POST approver Senior Director of Operations. POSTER approver SLT. INFO approver Senior Director of Operations. SIGN approver Senior Director of Operations.

> ANCHOR: Before any visual artifact is approved (first publication or any subsequent version), the artifact must be cross-checked against its source-of-truth document. Conflicts must be resolved before approval, not after.

# 14 Document Anatomy by Class

Required elements per class. All visual artifacts ship: editable .pptx, published .pdf, companion approval .docx in `/Visual Artifacts/Approval/`.
