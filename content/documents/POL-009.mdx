# Purpose and Scope

Keeping KitchFix systems, accounts, and data secure and used for their intended purpose.

This policy applies to every employee and contractor who uses KitchFix technology - devices, accounts, email, Slack, the intranet, Rippling, and any system holding KitchFix or client data. It pairs with AGR-002 Laptop Acceptance Agreement for issued hardware.

# Acceptable Use

Use KitchFix systems and accounts for KitchFix business, with good judgment.

Limited, reasonable personal use is acceptable as long as it does not interfere with work, security, or others.

Follow all KitchFix policies when using company systems - the Code of Conduct applies online too.

# Company Devices and Accounts

Issued devices are KitchFix property, used and cared for per AGR-002.

Keep software and devices updated. Do not disable security controls.

Return all devices and access on separation.

# Data Security and Confidentiality

Protect KitchFix and client data - especially player health, dietary, and personal information.

Store company records in approved systems, not personal accounts or drives.

Use strong, unique passwords and the access you are granted - do not share credentials.

Be alert to phishing and report anything suspicious immediately.

# Privacy and Monitoring

> NOTE: KitchFix systems and accounts are company property. Employees should have no expectation of privacy in their use of KitchFix systems, which may be monitored or accessed for legitimate business and security reasons, consistent with law.

# Prohibited Use

- No illegal, harassing, discriminatory, or threatening use.
- No accessing, sharing, or storing confidential data without authorization.
- No installing unauthorized software or connecting unapproved devices to company systems.
- No using company systems to compete with or harm KitchFix.

# Reporting and Consequences

Report lost or stolen devices, suspected breaches, and security concerns to the Senior Director of Operations immediately.

Violations are addressed through the Formal Disciplinary Process (SOP-004).

Acknowledgment is captured in Rippling.
