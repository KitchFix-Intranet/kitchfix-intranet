# 01 Why these are the Big Rules

Trust is what we sell. We are inside one of the most private workplaces in professional sports - the clubhouse. Players, coaches, and Club staff trust us to be there because we have earned that trust, every day, across every account.

These rules protect four people: the player, the Club, KitchFix, and you. Break any one of them and you put all four at risk.

These rules are not optional. They apply to every KitchFix employee - Executive Chef to dishwasher, full-time to seasonal - at every account, on every day, on and off the clock when in or near a Club facility.

> NOTE: AGR-001 supplements the KitchFix Employee Handbook (PB-004). Where these rules apply, they govern PFS conduct. Sign the Acknowledgement and Agreement (Section 09) before your first day on a Club site.

# 02 Confidentiality

This is the rule that anchors every other rule in this document. If you only remember one thing, remember this.

> ANCHOR: Everything in the clubhouse is confidential.

## What this means in practice

- Anything you see, hear, or learn inside a Club facility stays inside the Club facility.
- This includes - without limitation - roster moves and transactions, injuries and rehab status, lineup decisions, conversations between players and coaches, things you overhear in passing, personal information about players or their families, and any non-public business of the Club or the Baseball Organization.
- This applies whether or not the information is marked confidential. If you learned it because you were in the Club, it is confidential.
- This applies to your spouse, your friends, your family, and your group chats. There is no inner circle exception.
- This applies after you leave KitchFix. Confidentiality does not expire.
- If you are not sure whether something is confidential, treat it as confidential.

# 03 The Clubhouse Rules

These nine rules are the operator-facing version of how confidentiality, professionalism, and integrity actually show up in your shift.

## 3.1 No pictures or video recording

- No photos or videos of players, coaches, Club staff, or Club facilities while you are on Club premises.
- This includes the kitchen, dining areas, equipment rooms, and any spaces inside the Club facility.
- Operational photos required for the food service operation are the only exception, and only with Site Leader approval.
- Selfies in your KitchFix uniform inside a Club facility are not allowed.

## 3.2 No autographs

- Do not ask players, coaches, or Club staff for autographs.
- Do not ask anyone connected with the Club to sign anything for friends, family, or anyone else.
- This applies inside and outside the Club facility, in or out of uniform, on game days and off days.

## 3.3 No talk of gambling

- Do not bet on baseball games. Do not place bets through anyone else. Do not facilitate bets for anyone else.
- Do not discuss gambling, betting lines, prop bets, fantasy outcomes, or anything in that orbit with players, coaches, or Club staff.
- The full Major League Rule 21 governing gambling appears in Section 5. Read it. Sign it. Live by it.

## 3.4 No talk of steroid use

- Do not discuss, speculate about, ask about, or joke about player use of performance-enhancing substances.
- This applies to current players, former players, players on other teams, and prospects.
- If you hear something, confidentiality applies - see Section 2.

## 3.5 No talking to media or reporters

- You do not speak to media, reporters, podcasters, content creators, or anyone in a press capacity about anything connected to the Club, its players, its staff, or KitchFix's work for the Club.
- This includes off the record conversations. There is no off the record.
- If a reporter contacts you, refer them to the Club's communications team or to KitchFix leadership. Do not engage.

## 3.6 No inside information

- Anything you learn about Club operations is not yours to share - not with friends, not with family, not with anyone outside KitchFix and the Club.
- This includes trade discussions, lineup decisions, injury status, contract talks, prospect evaluations, and travel logistics.
- You do not share this information privately, in group chats, on social media, in DMs, or anywhere else.
- Sharing inside information for the purpose of betting - yours or someone else's - is a separate and more serious offense. See Section 5.

## 3.7 No dietary advice

- We are not registered dietitians. Do not give nutrition, supplement, weight management, or dietary advice to players - even if a player asks you directly, and even if you have known them for years.
- The Club's team dietitian is the only person authorized to give players dietary guidance.
- If a player asks you about food, ingredients, or how something was prepared, answer the food question. If they push into nutrition or dietary recommendations, refer them to the team dietitian.

## 3.8 No fraternizing with players and staff

- Do not develop personal relationships with Club players, coaches, or Club staff.
- Do not exchange personal contact information, follow each other on social media, accept invitations to personal events, or engage in social interactions outside the food service relationship.
- Friendly professional rapport is expected and welcomed. Personal friendships are not.

## 3.9 No soliciting

- Do not offer your services - culinary, personal chef, catering, training, or anything else - to players, coaches, or Club staff independently of KitchFix.
- Do not promote your own products, businesses, or side ventures inside a Club facility.
- Do not ask players, coaches, or Club staff for favors, introductions, endorsements, or anything else of personal benefit.

# 04 Facility Conduct

The Club's facility is the Club's house. Conduct yourself accordingly.

- You are subject to the Club's screening requirements at all times. This may include background checks, drug screens, and any other verification the Club requires.
- You wear Club-mandated credentials and identification at all times while on Club premises.
- No smoking, vaping, tobacco use, or alcohol consumption anywhere on Club facility premises.
- You follow all Club guidelines for parking, loading zones, and vehicle access.
- You comply with all Club facility rules and regulations - written AND unwritten. If a Club staff member tells you a rule, that rule applies to you.
- Club personnel may refuse you entry to the facility if you do not comply. If that happens, contact your supervisor immediately. Do not argue with Club staff.
- Do not enter areas designated for players, coaches, or Club staff only.

# 05 Integrity of the Game (Major League Rule 21)

Major League Rule 21 governs the conduct of anyone connected with a Club. As a PFS employee assigned to a Major League or Minor League Club, you are connected with the Club. The provisions below apply to you exactly as they apply to players, umpires, and Club personnel.

> ANCHOR: Read every word. This is the policy from MLB.

## Misconduct in Playing Baseball

Any player or person connected with a Club who shall promise or agree to lose, or to attempt to lose, or to fail to give his best efforts towards the winning of any baseball game with which he is or may be in any way concerned, or who shall intentionally lose or attempt to lose, or intentionally fail to give his best efforts towards the winning of any such baseball game, or who shall solicit or attempt to induce any player or person connected with a Club to lose or attempt to lose, or to fail to give his best efforts towards the winning of any baseball game with which such other player or person is or may be in any way concerned, or who, being solicited by any person, shall fail to inform the Commissioner immediately of such solicitation, and of all facts and circumstances connected therewith, shall be declared permanently ineligible.

## Gift for Defeating Competing Club

Any player or person connected with a Club who shall offer or give any gift or reward to a player or person connected with another Club for services rendered or supposed to be or to have been rendered in defeating or attempting to defeat a competing Club shall be declared ineligible for not less than three years.

## Gifts to Umpires

Any player or person connected with a Club who shall give, or offer to give, any gift or reward to an umpire for services rendered, or supposed to be or to have been rendered, in defeating or attempting to defeat a competing Club, or for the umpire's decision on anything connected with the playing of a baseball game, shall be declared permanently ineligible.

## Gambling

Any player, umpire, or Club or League official or employee, who shall bet any sum whatsoever upon any baseball game in connection with which the bettor has no duty to perform, shall be declared ineligible for one year.

Any player, umpire, or Club or League official or employee, who shall bet any sum whatsoever upon any baseball game in connection with which the bettor has a duty to perform, shall be declared permanently ineligible.

Any player, umpire, or Club or League official or employee who places bets with illegal book makers, or agents for illegal book makers, shall be subject to such penalty as the Commissioner deems appropriate.

## Violence or Misconduct

In case of any physical attack or other violence upon an umpire by a player, or by an umpire upon a player, the Commissioner shall impose upon the offender such fine, suspension, ineligibility or other penalty, as the facts may warrant.

## Other Misconduct

Nothing herein contained shall be construed as exclusively defining or otherwise limiting acts, transactions, practices or conduct not to be in the best interests of Baseball; and any and all other acts, transactions, practices or conduct not to be in the best interests of Baseball are prohibited and shall be subject to such penalties, including permanent ineligibility, as the facts in the particular case may warrant.

# 06 Communication and Escalation

All work issues - scheduling, staffing, equipment, food cost, conflict, performance concerns, anything - go to KitchFix leadership first.

> CRITICAL: You do not raise KitchFix issues with the Client. Ever.

## Standard escalation chain

1. Your direct supervisor (Sous Chef, Hospitality Manager, Executive Chef, or General Manager depending on your role).
2. The Executive Chef on site.
3. Your Regional Director of Operations (RDO).
4. Senior Director of Operations.
5. VP of Operations.
6. People Operations.

## HR-related issues

Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, and safety concerns can be raised directly to People Operations at any point in the chain.

If you are in doubt about who to escalate to, contact your RDO or People Operations.

# 07 What You CAN Do

One thing you can say publicly. Beyond that, the rules above govern.

You may state on your resume, in a job interview, or in a professional context that you worked for KitchFix Performance Food Service and which Club(s) you supported during your employment.

That is the only public statement about your work for the Club that is permitted. Anything beyond confirming the working relationship is governed by the rules above.

# 08 What Happens When Rules Are Broken

Consequences scale with the rule and the breach. Severity follows the document hierarchy.

- **Facility Conduct breaches (Section 4)** - may result in immediate removal from the Club facility by Club staff or by KitchFix. Repeated or willful breaches result in termination of employment.
- **Confidentiality breaches (Section 2)** - result in termination of employment. They may also result in legal action by KitchFix, the Club, or the Baseball Organization.
- **Clubhouse Rule breaches (Section 3)** - range from corrective conversation to termination of employment, depending on severity. Fraternization, soliciting, autographs, and unauthorized media contact are typically termination offenses on first occurrence.
- **Communication and Escalation breaches (Section 6)** - result in a corrective conversation on first occurrence. Repeated breaches may result in termination.
- **Integrity of the Game breaches (Section 5 / Major League Rule 21)** - result in termination of employment AND permanent ineligibility from baseball as imposed by the Commissioner of Baseball or the President of the Minor League Association. These penalties are not discretionary on KitchFix's part.

KitchFix retains the right to take any disciplinary action up to and including termination at its discretion, consistent with the at-will employment relationship described in the KitchFix Employee Handbook.
