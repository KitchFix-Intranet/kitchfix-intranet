### B.3 Service fee (structure and installment schedule)

> "Services Fee. During the Term (defined below), Team shall pay Provider a services fee (the "Services Fee") in the amount set forth in each SOW. The Services Fee shall be comprised of a per-meal fee ("Per-Meal Fee"), and such other fees or charges as may be set forth on the applicable SOW. Unless otherwise provided in the SOW, following payment of the Annual Deposit (defined below), Team shall receive a discount equal to 20% of the Per-Meal Fee for each meal provided pursuant to this Agreement and the applicable SOW."

[Source: Texas Rangers 2025-2027 Surprise Food Service Agreement.pdf § 2.a Services Fee p.1]

> "Annual Deposit. Unless otherwise set forth in any SOW, annually prior to the start of each year of the Term (including any Renewal Term), Team shall pay to Provider an amount equal to 20% of the total Services Fee, calculated based upon a projected number of daily meals (the "Projection") under all active SOWs for the then current year of the Term, which amount shall represent a deposit on the Term's Services (the "Annual Deposit"). For the Initial Term (defined below) and any Renewals Terms (defined below), the Annual Deposit shall be due and payable in three equal installments on the first day of January, February and March of the applicable contract year."

[Source: Texas Rangers 2025-2027 Surprise Food Service Agreement.pdf § 2.b Annual Deposit p.1]

> "Total 2025 Annual Deposit Due - $297,419.26, not inclusive of sales tax, if applicable
> Due January 1st, 2025 - $99,139.75, not inclusive of sales tax, if applicable
> Due February 1st, 2025 - $99,139.75, not inclusive of sales tax, if applicable
> Due March 1st, 2025 - $99,139.75, not inclusive of sales tax, if applicable"

[Source: Texas Rangers 2025-2027 Surprise Food Service Agreement.pdf § SOW#1 p.11]

2026 Annual Deposit dollar amount and installment schedule: NOT PRESENT (no 2026 SOW).

## §D. Cross-check flags

- 2026 SOW paperwork gap: No 2026 SOW is on file for TXR-AZ. The 2025-2027 master says "Services Fee ... in the amount set forth in each SOW" (§2.a p.1) and "Annual Deposit ... calculated based upon a projected number of daily meals ... under all active SOWs for the then current year" (§2.b p.1). Absent a 2026 SOW, the 2026 Annual Deposit amount and per-meal dollar rates are not documented in the contract - only the 2.5% escalation formula applied to 2025 rates. Confirm against Phase 0a Q4 flag and any actual 2026 invoices.
- 2025 non-MLB SKUs shrunk vs. 2022: 2022 SOW included "Continental breakfast - $8" as a separate SKU; the 2025 SOW omits it. Cross-check MONEY_MODEL / PG for how Continental is currently priced in 2026.
- Kitchen equipment "up to $75,000" appears in both agreements as a fresh Provider commitment. Verify KitchFix has not already funded this in 2022 and been asked to fund again under 2025-2027.
- Start-up Fee of $50,000 in 2022 does NOT appear in 2025-2027 (no §2.a Start-Up Fee), which is consistent with a rollover but flag if EVIDENCE pack still lists it.
- Team signatory title change (VP/AGM to General Manager) between 2022 and 2025 signatures does not affect governance but note for cross-signature audits.
