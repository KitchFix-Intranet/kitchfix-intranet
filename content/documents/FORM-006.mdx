# 05 Access Revocation

The following access must be revoked the same day as separation. Check when complete:

- KitchFix intranet / People Portal access revoked
- Email account deactivated
- Rippling access removed
- Google Workspace access removed
- Site-specific access (keys, security codes) revoked

# 06 Final Pay & Benefits

People Operations is responsible for final pay processing in compliance with applicable state law. The manager completes this section to provide the relevant information.

- Final pay period end date
- Unpaid hours or outstanding pay items
- Accrued PTO owed (if applicable)
- Benefits continuation notes

> NOTE: State law governs final pay timing. Contact People Operations immediately. Do not make verbal commitments to the employee about final pay timing.
