# Purpose

Complete this form when an employee or other affected person declines offered medical treatment following a workplace injury or incident. Submit to Human Resources within 24 hours. The completed form lives in HR Confidential storage.

> ANCHOR: An offered-and-refused treatment must be documented. The refusal protects the employee's right to make the decision and protects KitchFix from a downstream claim that treatment was withheld.

# When to Use

- An employee declines offered transport to clinic, urgent care, or 911.
- An employee declines on-site first aid following a reportable injury.
- A non-employee (player, client, vendor, visitor) on KitchFix-controlled premises declines offered medical care.

Reference: SOP-002 section 7.1 Employee Injury or Illness and SOP-002 Appendix C Refusal Workflow.

# Form Contents

The form captures the following sections. Field-level data is collected at signing event; this MDX records the canonical refusal statement language only.

## 1 - Affected Person

Full Name, Role / Position, Site / Location.

## 2 - Incident Details

Date, Time, Location of incident, Description of injury or incident.

## 3 - Treatment Offered

Treatment offered (911, on-site medical, urgent care, first aid, etc.), Offered by (name and role).

## 4 - Refusal Statement (canonical text)

I, the undersigned, acknowledge that medical treatment was offered to me by KitchFix or by site medical personnel following the incident described above. I understand the nature of the treatment offered and the potential risks of declining. I voluntarily refuse the offered treatment and release KitchFix Performance Food Service, its employees, and the Club from liability arising from this decision. I understand I may seek treatment on my own at any time; this decision does not waive that right.

## 5 - Signatures

Affected Person (Printed Name, Signature, Date) and Witness - KitchFix Representative (Printed Name and Role, Signature, Date).

## 6 - Human Resources Receipt

Received by (HR name and signature), Date received, HR case ID.

# Submission

Submit completed form to Human Resources within 24 hours of the incident. HR enters the case ID and stores the original. Submit through the People Portal Incident Center or in person to HR.
