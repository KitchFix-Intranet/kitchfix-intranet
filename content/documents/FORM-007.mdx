# HOW TO USE

## Using This Form

One form per employee, per increase. It carries a pay-increase recommendation through approval and into Rippling. Use it alongside POL-007 (how increases are decided) and the pay-band references REF-006 (hourly) and REF-007 (leadership).

Route in order:

- **Recommend** - the Executive Chef (hourly) or RDO (leadership) completes The Request and the rationale.
- **Approve** - the increase is approved per the POL-007 authority matrix.
- **Validate** - People Operations validates band, equity, and compliance; Accounting validates budget fit.
- **Enter** - once approved and validated, the increase is entered in Rippling with its effective date, and this form is filed with People Operations.

> NOTE: An increase cannot be entered until both validations are complete. If the employee is on an active disciplinary track under SOP-004, the increase is paused until it is resolved.

# THE REQUEST

## The Request

Completed by the recommending leader.

- **Employee Name**
- **Role**
- **Account / State**
- **Increase Type** - Merit - Market / Structural - Promotion - Equity - Retention
- **Current Rate**
- **Current Band Position** - Lower third - Midpoint - Upper third
- **Proposed New Rate**
- **New Band Position** - Lower third - Midpoint - Upper third
- **% Increase**
- **Effective Date** - Season return offer, or a specific date
- **Performance Review on File** - Type + date - SOP-007 (hourly) or SOP-001 (leadership)
- **Disciplinary Status** - None / Active. An active Level 3, final warning, or PIP (SOP-004) pauses the increase.
- **Certifications Current** - ServSafe Food Handler + Allergen - Y / N
- **Within Band?** - Y / N. If N, VP Ops + People Operations exception sign-off is required.
- **Rationale / Justification**

# APPROVALS & VALIDATION

## Approvals & Validation

Signatures route per the POL-007 authority matrix. People Operations and Accounting both validate before Rippling entry.

- **Recommended By - EC / RDO** - Name - signature - date
- **Approved By - RDO / VP Ops** - Name - signature - date
- **People Ops Validation** - Band, equity, compliance. Name - date
- **Accounting Validation** - Budget fit against account plan. Name - date
- **Entered in Rippling** - Date - entered by

# HANDLING & REFERENCES

## Handling & References

> CRITICAL: Restricted. A completed form contains confidential pay information. It is shared only along the approval chain and filed with People Operations. It is not left in shared site folders or discussed with other employees.
