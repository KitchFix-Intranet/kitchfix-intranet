# Purpose

Complete this worksheet at the scene of any KitchFix or business-use vehicle incident. Captures the facts before memory fades. The formal incident report in the People Portal follows within the SOP-002 SLA for the severity tier.

Per SOP-002 section 7.2: any injury escalates the incident to S1 and a 911 call. Vehicle incidents are notified to the VP of Operations regardless of severity.

# Form Contents

## 1 - Incident Details

Date, Time, Exact location (street, intersection, or facility), Weather and road conditions.

## 2 - KitchFix Vehicle

Vehicle ID or plate, Make / Model / Year, KitchFix Driver (Printed Name, Role, License number), Site / Account.

## 3 - Other Vehicle (if any)

Plate, Make / Model / Year, Driver Printed Name, Driver License number, Driver phone, Driver insurance carrier and policy.

## 4 - Police and Authorities

911 called (Yes / No), Police on scene (Yes / No), Officer name and badge, Police report number, Tow service called (Yes / No).

## 5 - Witnesses

Witness 1: Name, Phone, Statement summary. Witness 2: Name, Phone, Statement summary.

## 6 - Photos

Photos of: all vehicles, all plates, damage from multiple angles, scene context, road conditions, any visible injury. Attach photos to the People Portal incident report.

## 7 - Immediate Notifications Made

Notified VP of Operations (Yes / No, time), Notified HR (Yes / No, time), Notified Site Leader (Yes / No, time).

## 8 - Factual Narrative

Plain description of what happened. No speculation. No admission of fault.

## 9 - Signatures

KitchFix Driver: Printed Name, Signature, Date. Site Leader or supervisor: Printed Name, Signature, Date.

# Important - what NOT to do at scene

- Do not admit fault.
- Do not speculate on cause.
- Do not offer compensation or settle anything at the scene.
- Do not discuss the incident with the other party beyond exchanging required information.

# Submission

This worksheet plus photos goes to Human Resources within 24 hours of the incident. The formal incident report follows in the People Portal per SOP-002 section 7.2.
