## §B. Operative terms - VERBATIM (visitors'-clubhouse-specific clauses within 2026 MLB Food Services Agreement)

### B.1 Term / duration

Same as TXR-TX-H: Effective Jan 21, 2026, expires Dec 31, 2026.

[Source: Food_Services_Agreement_-_KitchFix_(MLB_2026).pdf § 3.a p.2 and preamble p.1]

### B.2 Per-meal rates

NOT PRESENT for visiting clubhouse. The contract does not price visitors'-clubhouse services as separate meals; they fall under the fixed Services Fee together with home services.

### B.3 Service fee (amount, structure)

Visiting-clubhouse services are NOT separately fee'd. They are bundled into the single $604,032.00 Services Fee.

> "Services Fee. During the Term (defined below), the Rangers shall pay Contractor $604,032.00 (the 'Services Fee') as payment in full for the Services and all ingredients and supplies required for Meal preparation, delivery, service and clean-up."

[Source: Food_Services_Agreement_-_KitchFix_(MLB_2026).pdf § 2.a p.1]

The scope of the Services covered by that fee explicitly includes visitors'-clubhouse offerings:

> "In addition to the Services and Meals, Contractor agrees to provide the following additional 'daily offerings': made-to-order options during the first two meals of every Rangers' home game; Grab & Go Snack options made by Contractor; packaged snacks, condiments, and beverages; and coffee service. Further, in the visitors' clubhouse, Contractor agrees to provide: Grab & Go Snack options made by Contractor; packaged snacks, condiments, and beverages; and coffee service."

[Source: Food_Services_Agreement_-_KitchFix_(MLB_2026).pdf § 1.b Meals p.1]

Location of service:

> "The Meals shall be served in the Rangers' home clubhouse (and the visiting clubhouse when appropriate), unless the parties mutually agree upon another area."

[Source: Food_Services_Agreement_-_KitchFix_(MLB_2026).pdf § 1.c Preparation and Service of Meals in Globe Life Field p.1]

## §D. Cross-check flags

- Paperwork gap: No standalone visiting-clubhouse contract or SOW exists. Visitors'-clubhouse services are a limited scope carve-in ("Grab & Go Snack options made by Contractor; packaged snacks, condiments, and beverages; and coffee service") within the master 2026 MLB agreement, and no dollar amount, meal count, or discrete revenue line is assignable to TXR-TX-V from the contract alone.
- The visitors'-clubhouse carve-in expressly EXCLUDES made-to-order and buffet service (which is a Home-only offering per § 1.b "made-to-order options during the first two meals of every Rangers' home game"). Any Price Review v3 or MONEY_MODEL that credits TXR-TX-V with hot-meal / buffet revenue is out of scope per contract.
- Location clause (§ 1.c) says Meals "shall be served in the Rangers' home clubhouse (and the visiting clubhouse when appropriate)" - which is ambiguous about whether visiting clubhouse gets hot meals at manager's discretion. Flag for verbal-practice check vs. contract.
- If EVIDENCE / MONEY_MODEL splits TXR-TX-H vs TXR-TX-V revenue, the allocation is not derivable from contract - it must come from a separate cost-accounting basis and should be flagged as internal allocation, not contract-driven.
- No 2026 visiting-clubhouse SOW or amendment on file; if the Rangers ever expanded visitors'-clubhouse service (e.g., added hot meals) after Jan 21, 2026, that expansion is undocumented - flag paperwork gap.
