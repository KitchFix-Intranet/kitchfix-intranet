# 01 About and How to Use

What this Handbook is. Where it sits in the document system. How to use it.

## 1.1 About This Handbook

The SLA OS Handbook defines the structure every KitchFix account agreement follows. Ten sections, applied consistently, so that every account is described in the same operating language.

This Handbook is the template - the shell. The filled SLAs (Cardinals PDC, Reds PDC, Rangers MLB, and so on) are standalone account documents built against this template. When this Handbook is updated, the structure of every active SLA is updated; the content of each filled SLA is the account-specific working detail.

> ANCHOR: The Handbook is not the contract. It is the working document that organizes the contract, the operational standards, and the account-level overrides into a single reference. It governs how the relationship runs day to day, season over season.

## 1.2 How to Use

Account-level overrides - KitchFix Level SOPs (Allergen Playbook, ServSafe standards, menu approval workflow, communication conventions) apply at every account by default. Account-specific overrides are documented explicitly in the filled SLA - never implicitly assumed.

Updates - The SLA is reviewed at every ABR. Material changes during the year are documented as amendments and incorporated at the next ABR. The SLA is a living document, but changes route through the documented review process - not by handshake.

Distribution - The signed SLA lives in shared KitchFix systems accessible to the VPO, RDO, Site Leader, and authorized corporate staff. The client receives a current version on a documented cadence.

## 1.3 Companion Documents

The SLA OS does not stand alone. It works with:

- **PB-001 Leadership OS Handbook** - The leadership and operating system. SLAs are managed by the leadership system; this Handbook supplies the SLA structure.
- **AGR-001 The Big Rules - Confidentiality** - Referenced in section 6 Communication, Confidentiality, and Conduct. The Big Rules are non-negotiable conduct standards that bind every KitchFix team member at every account.
- **PB-002 Allergen Playbook** - Referenced in section 5 Nutrition Guidelines and Allergen Standards. The Allergen Playbook is the company-wide standard. Account-level overrides are documented explicitly in the filled SLA.
- **SOP-001 Leadership Performance System** - Connects to section 7 Performance and Accountability. Internal performance reviews (Cycle Review, WOW Plan) are governed by SOP-001; client-facing reviews are governed by section 7 below.

# 02 The Ten-Section Structure

Every account SLA is built against this ten-section structure. Every section appears in every filled SLA. Account-specific content fills the structure; the structure does not change.

| Section | What It Covers |
|---|---|
| 1 - Commercial Terms | Contractual foundation. Parties, term, renewal, termination, force majeure, pricing model, insurance, IP. |
| 2 - Scope of Services | What KitchFix provides, what the client provides, what is out of scope, vehicles, transportation. |
| 3 - Service Calendar | Annual operating calendar. Per-day service profile. Schedule change process. |
| 4 - Operational SOP | How KitchFix operates the account day to day. Menu approval. Meal specifications. Service formats. Change management. |
| 5 - Nutrition Guidelines and Allergen Standards | Nutrition philosophy and guidelines. Portion standards. Allergen position. Severe allergy and emergency response. |
| 6 - Communication, Confidentiality, and Conduct | Points of contact. Communication cadence. Escalation paths. Confidentiality. Professional conduct standards. |
| 7 - Performance and Accountability | How performance against the SLA is measured, reviewed, and remediated. Audits. Client-facing reviews. Surveys. |
| 8 - Specialty and Custom | Outside catering. Special events. Latin cuisine commitment. Food holidays and cultural meals. |
| 9 - Operational Standards | Day-to-day operating bar. Service times. Staffing. Equipment. Sanitation. Certifications. Region-specific protocols. |
| 10 - Financials and Reporting | Financial model designation. Period-end reporting. Year-end reporting. Billing reconciliation. Audit rights. |

> NOTE: Sections 3-10 may carry the most account-specific weight, but all ten sections are required in every filled SLA. A section with no account-specific overrides should explicitly state "KitchFix Level standards apply" - not be left blank.

# 03 Section 1 - Commercial Terms

Contractual foundation. Reviewed by legal before execution. Updated at renewal.

- **Parties** - Legal entities of both sides. CJK Foods LLC dba KitchFix and the client legal entity. Authorized signatories named for both sides.
- **Term and renewal** - Initial term length. Renewal terms. Annual SLA Review (ABR) date - the structured contract-level conversation that runs every year.
- **Termination** - Termination for convenience and termination for cause. Material breach definition. Cure period. Notice requirements. Transition support.
- **Force majeure** - Force majeure events and obligations. Communication requirements during a force majeure event. Continuation or suspension of services.
- **Pricing model** - Fee model or full-service. Per-meal rates or fixed fees. Service fee structure. Pricing adjustment clause - annual escalator, CPI tied, or negotiated at ABR.
- **Insurance and liability** - Insurance carriers and coverage levels held by KitchFix. Insurance requirements held by the client. Indemnification.
- **Intellectual property and data rights** - Recipe IP and ownership. Menu IP. Photo and footage rights inside the facility. Data ownership for nutrition data, meal counts, and operational metrics.

# 04 Section 2 - Scope of Services

What KitchFix provides, what the client provides, and what is explicitly out of scope.

- **KitchFix provides** - Services delivered. Points of service (clubhouse, dining room, weight room, training room, suite). Equipment KitchFix brings. Staffing structure.
- **Client provides** - Facility access including kitchen, dining, and storage spaces. Beverages, smoothie ingredients, coffee program (where applicable). Utilities. Cleaning chemicals.
- **Visitor and overflow services** - Visiting team service (where applicable). Front office events. Suite catering. Road trip catering. Cross-facility coverage.
- **Vehicle and transportation** - KitchFix vehicles operating on client property. Food transport between facilities. Driver requirements, insurance, and vehicle standards.
- **Exclusions** - Explicitly out of scope. Items historically requested but not included. Items priced separately as add-ons. Anything that requires a change order.

# 05 Section 3 - Service Calendar

The annual operating calendar covers every operating day of the year, with per-day service profile and pricing.

- **Annual calendar** - Every operating day is documented. Spring Training, regular season, post-season (where applicable), off-season, holidays, blackout periods.
- **Per-day service profile** - Meal types by day (breakfast, arrival, BP, pregame, postgame, snack, fueling station). Per-meal rate or fixed-fee allocation.
- **Schedule changes** - Process for documenting schedule changes during the season. Authority to commit to changes (Site Leader, RDO, VPO). Pricing impact.

# 06 Section 4 - Operational SOP

How KitchFix operates the account day to day. Account-specific operating standards that build on KitchFix Level SOPs.

- **Menu development and approval** - Approval workflow: SLA + Director of Culinary + Dietitian + CHM (where applicable). Menu cycle length. Revision cadence. Latin every meal period.
- **Meal specifications by period** - Breakfast, arrival, BP, pregame, postgame, snack profiles. Hot food and cold food balance. Carbohydrate, protein, vegetable composition.
- **Service formats** - Made-to-order standards. Action stations and what each station offers. Salad bar. Grab and Go. Pre-packaged products. Beverage program.
- **Change management** - How operational changes are requested, evaluated, approved, and documented. Who approves on each side. Documentation trail.

# 07 Section 5 - Nutrition Guidelines and Allergen Standards

Nutrition philosophy and guidelines, portion standards, allergen handling, and emergency response protocols. The Allergen Playbook is the company-wide standard.

- **Nutrition Guidelines** - Account-specific nutrition guidelines or team document reference. Dietitian-led directives. Performance nutrition framework.
- **Portion standards** - Per-meal portion guidelines. Athlete-specific portioning (where applicable). Plating and service standards.
- **Allergen position** - KitchFix Allergen Playbook applies in full at every account. The Allergen Playbook is the single source of truth for Top 9 allergen handling, severe-allergy escalation, and emergency response.
- **Account-level allergen overrides** - Any account-level deviations from the Allergen Playbook documented explicitly here, with the rationale and the approving authority on both sides.
- **Severe allergy and emergency response** - Severe allergy escalation protocol. Anaphylaxis response. Emergency contacts (client medical staff, KitchFix Site Leader, RDO).
- **Cooking methods** - Approved cooking methods. Restricted cooking methods. Approved fats and oils. Restricted ingredients. Foods to limit or avoid.

> CRITICAL: The Allergen Playbook is the single source of truth for allergen handling. Account-level overrides must be explicit and documented. Implicit deviations are not permitted.

# 08 Section 6 - Communication, Confidentiality, and Conduct

How KitchFix and the client communicate, what stays inside the facility, and the standards of professional conduct every KitchFix team member is expected to meet.

- **Points of contact** - Client-side: primary contact, secondary contact, escalation contact. KitchFix-side: Site Leader, RDO, VPO. Roles, responsibilities, response standards.
- **Communication cadence** - Bi-weekly RDO + SL + Client check-in (minimum). Daily operational communication channels. Weekly status reporting (where applicable).
- **Escalation paths** - Operational escalation: SL to RDO to VPO. Commercial escalation: VPO to CEO. Emergency escalation: defined by issue type (food safety, security, medical).
- **Confidentiality** - All information inside the client facility is confidential. Player health, performance, contract status, personal information. AGR-001 The Big Rules in full.
- **Standards of professional conduct** - KitchFix team standards inside the facility. AGR-001 The Big Rules: no photos, no autographs, no fraternization, no media engagement.

> ANCHOR: AGR-001 The Big Rules govern conduct at every account. The Big Rules are signed by every KitchFix team member on hire and reinforced through Big Rules postings at every site.

# 09 Section 7 - Performance and Accountability

How performance against the SLA is measured, reviewed, and remediated. The structure that makes the SLA enforceable rather than aspirational.

- **Performance measurement** - Operational audits - self-audits and corporate audits, two waves per year per contract type. Audit rubric tied to SLA standards.
- **Client-facing reviews** - Pre-ST Review or Pre-Season Review (~2 weeks before ramp). Post-ST Review or Post-Season Review (within 30 days of close). ABR/SLA Review. EOY Review.
- **Client surveys** - Survey cadence. Survey content areas (food quality, service, hospitality, communication, alignment). How survey responses feed quarterly reviews.
- **Remediation** - How performance gaps are addressed. Corrective action timelines. Escalation if remediation does not produce change. Dispute resolution.
- **Performance reporting** - What performance data the client receives, in what format, on what cadence. Audit summaries shared (or not shared). Confidentiality of internal scoring.

# 10 Section 8 - Specialty and Custom

Account-specific service offerings outside the standard operating profile.

- **Outside catering** - Definition of outside catering. Approval requirements. Notice requirements. Pricing. Coordination with KitchFix operations.
- **Special events** - Family days, kids' nights, Fun Money events, holiday meals, Opening Day, post-season qualifying days, retirement celebrations.
- **Latin cuisine commitment** - Latin every meal period - KitchFix non-negotiable applies at every account. Account-specific Latin program details documented here.
- **Food holidays and cultural meals** - Holiday meal programming. Cultural and religious dietary accommodations. Themed days or weeks. Approval workflow.

# 11 Section 9 - Operational Standards

The day-to-day operating bar at the account. What the client can expect to walk into every day, every shift, every meal period.

- **Service times** - Meal period start and end times. Pre-shift timing. Service window enforcement. Account-specific service time expectations.
- **Opening and closing standards** - Daily opening protocols. Daily closing protocols. End-of-week, end-of-homestand, and end-of-cycle close procedures.
- **Staffing** - Staffing minimums by meal period. Salaried leadership presence requirements. Hourly staffing ratios. Coverage during peak, ramp, and off-cycle windows.
- **Equipment** - Equipment standards and operating expectations. KitchFix-owned equipment. Client-owned equipment. Maintenance responsibility split.
- **Sanitation and pest control** - Daily sanitation standards. Cleaning schedules. Sanitizer concentration logs. Pest control vendor and inspection cadence.
- **Certifications and MSDS** - ServSafe currency requirements (Food Managers, Allergens, Food Handlers). Other certifications required by the facility or jurisdiction.
- **Health inspection and regulatory cooperation** - Health department inspection response protocol. Notification chain when an inspector arrives.
- **Facility access and security** - Key and badge management. Vendor escort requirements. Restricted area protocols. Visitor and guest policy for KitchFix staff.
- **Branding, signage, and presentation** - Brand-compliant signage in the dining area. Allergen labeling at every meal period. Nutritional information at every meal.
- **End-of-season transition** - Shutdown checklist execution. Equipment storage. Inventory close-out. Vendor close-out. Document archive. Facility handoff.
- **Sustainability and environmental commitments** - Composting, sourcing, packaging, and single-use plastic commitments where applicable. Account-specific commitments captured here.
- **Region-specific protocols** - Geographic protocols that bear on day-to-day operations: hurricane preparedness (Florida), earthquake protocols (where applicable), severe weather response.

# 12 Section 10 - Financials and Reporting

The financial operating model and the reporting cadence that supports it.

- **Financial model designation** - Fee model or full-service. Account-specific labor formula (e.g., TBJ hourly labor target). Revenue base calculation.
- **Period-end reporting** - Period-end financial review participants - Site Leader, AP, RDO. Reporting deliverables. Variance commentary. Account-facing summaries.
- **Year-end reporting** - Year-end financial review participants - Site Leader, AP, RDO. Annual deliverables. Inputs to renewal preparation.
- **Billing reconciliation** - Billing cycle. Reconciliation process. Dispute resolution timeline. Documentation requirements. Signing authority for invoices.
- **Change-order pricing** - How scope changes get priced. Approval thresholds. Documentation requirements. Time-and-materials versus fixed-fee adjustments.
- **Audit rights** - Client audit rights for financial records. KitchFix's right to audit client-provided inputs (meal counts, headcounts, schedules).

# 13 Account SLAs

Every active KitchFix account has an SLA built against the template above. The filled SLAs live as standalone documents.

The Handbook supplies the structure; the filled SLAs hold the account-specific working detail. Active SLAs are maintained by the Senior Director of Operations and reviewed annually at ABR. The current set is linked from the KitchFix Playbook directory.

> NOTE: When the Handbook is updated, the structure of every active SLA is updated. When a filled SLA is updated mid-year as an amendment, the next ABR formalizes the amendment into the body of the SLA.
