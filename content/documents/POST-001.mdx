# Incident Reporting - Posted Summary

A wall-mounted reference for every kitchen and office. The binding text is SOP-002 Safety and Incident Management. This posting is the at-a-glance reference for severity tiers, notification windows, and the six-step response.

## When something happens

> CRITICAL: If life-safety risk exists, call 911 first. Documentation comes after.

## The four severity tiers

- **S1 Life-Safety / Regulatory** - immediate threat to life or regulatory exposure. Anaphylaxis, loss of consciousness, seizure, severe bleeding, vehicle accident with injury, suspected foodborne illness in 2+ individuals.
- **S2 Medical / Significant** - medical attention beyond first aid; lost-time potential. Cut requiring stitches, burn requiring clinic, sprain or fracture, vehicle damage with no injury.
- **S3 First Aid / Minor** - resolved on-site; no lost time. Minor cut or burn, slip with no injury, cross-contact caught before service.
- **S4 Near-Miss / Hazard** - no harm occurred; conditions or near-event noted.

## The six-step response

1. **Respond** - immediate safety; call 911 if life-threatening.
2. **Stabilize** - remove hazard; pull contaminated food and utensils.
3. **Notify** - per the matrix above.
4. **Document** - People Portal Incident Center within the SLA.
5. **Investigate** - within 48 hours, Manager of Record plus HR.
6. **Close** - corrective action in 7 days; 30-day check-in on injuries.
