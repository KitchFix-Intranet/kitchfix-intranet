# A Note from Chef Josh

Welcome to KitchFix.

You joined a team that feeds and takes care of some of the best athletes in professional sports. That is not a small thing. What you do every day - preparing food, serving with a smile, keeping the kitchen clean and running - directly affects how those athletes perform and how they feel about the people taking care of them.

We don't need you to be perfect. We need you to show up, work hard, treat people right, and take pride in what you do. If you do those things, you will fit right in here.

This handbook tells you what we expect and what you can expect from us. Read it. If something is unclear, ask your manager.

We're glad you're here.

Chef Josh Katt - Founder & CEO, KitchFix

# 01 Who We Are

KitchFix is a Performance Food Service company. We run the kitchens and dining rooms for professional sports organizations - MLB clubhouses, player development centers, and more.

> ANCHOR: Best Food. Best Service. Best Hospitality. That is our promise to every client and every player we serve. It starts with you.

We were founded in 2012 by Chef Josh Katt in Chicago. Today we operate across Florida, Texas, Arizona, New York, and Illinois. Every account we run is a high-stakes environment where the people we feed are performing at the highest level of their profession. They notice everything - the food, the cleanliness, the energy in the room, and the people serving them.

## What We Believe

- Humility. We support each other and get better every day.
- Intentionality. We grow with purpose and take pride in our craft.
- Sustainability. We build something that lasts.
- Equity. We treat everyone with fairness and respect.

## Our Hospitality Promise

Three things define every KitchFix shift:

- Best Food - chef-prepared, nutritious, and made with care.
- Best Service - flawless execution, every time.
- Best Hospitality - every person who walks into our space feels taken care of.

# 02 Your First Days

Here's what happens when you start and what you need to do to be ready.

## Before Your First Shift

- Complete your onboarding paperwork through Rippling. Your manager will send you the link.
- Read and sign The Big Rules (AGR-001). This is required before you can work.
- Read this handbook. You will be asked to confirm you received it.
- Make sure your uniform is complete and compliant. See Section 04.

## Required Certifications

All KitchFix hourly employees are required to hold two certifications:

- ServSafe Food Handler Certification. This is a nationally recognized food safety certification. If you do not already have it, your manager will direct you to complete it. It must be current at all times.
- ServSafe Allergen Certification. This covers the top allergens, how to handle them, and what to do if a guest has a reaction. Required for all employees who handle or serve food.

> NOTE: Both certifications must be completed before or shortly after your first shift, as directed by your manager. KitchFix will provide guidance on how to complete them. You are responsible for keeping them current.

## Your First Week

Your manager will walk you through the site, introduce you to the team, and show you how things run at your account. Ask questions. Every site is a little different and there is no expectation that you will know everything on day one.

What you are expected to know from day one: show up on time in full uniform, follow food safety rules, and treat every player, staff member, and teammate with respect.

# 03 Showing Up

Reliability is one of the most important things you bring to this job. Your teammates and your clients count on you.

> ANCHOR: When you don't show up, someone else covers your work. That affects the whole team and it affects the players we serve. We take attendance seriously.

## Schedule

Your schedule is posted in Rippling. Check it regularly. Your manager sets the schedule based on the needs of the account.

## If You Are Going to Be Late or Absent

Contact your manager as soon as you know - before your shift starts. Do not just not show up. A no-call no-show is one of the most serious attendance violations and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Overtime

Overtime must be approved by your manager in advance. Do not assume you can stay late without approval. If your manager asks you to work overtime, you will be compensated according to applicable law.

## Clocking In & Out

Always clock in and out accurately in Rippling. Do not clock in early or stay clocked in after your shift ends without manager approval. If you notice an error in your hours, tell your manager immediately - do not wait until payday.

# 04 Appearance & Uniform

You represent KitchFix every moment you are on client property. Your uniform is part of that.

Arrive for every shift in full, clean, compliant uniform. The full uniform standard for your role is in POL-002 Appearance & Dress Code Policy. Your manager will walk you through what you specifically need to wear at your account.

## The Basics

- Black, close-toed, non-slip shoes. Always.
- Clean, well-fitted black pants or dark jeans - no rips, no acid-wash, no embellishments.
- Your KitchFix shirt, polo, or chef coat - clean and pressed.
- Your KitchFix hat or approved team hat.
- No headphones or earbuds. Ever. This is a food safety and guest experience rule.
- No jewelry except a single wedding band.
- No cologne or perfume. Scents interfere with food quality.

KitchFix provides your initial uniform items. You provide your shoes, pants, undershirts, and socks.

# 05 Food Safety

Food safety is not optional. It is the foundation of everything we do.

> CRITICAL: A food safety violation that endangers the health of a player or guest is grounds for immediate termination. There are no exceptions.

> NOTE: All employees are required to hold a current ServSafe Food Handler Certification and a current ServSafe Allergen Certification. See Section 02 for details.

## The Rules That Never Change

- Wash your hands. After the restroom, after handling raw food, after touching your face, after breaks. Every time. Soap and hot water. No shortcuts.
- If you are sick - vomiting, diarrhea, jaundice, or a fever - do not come in. Call your manager. You will not be penalized for staying home when you are genuinely sick.
- No nail polish, false nails, or nail adornments when handling food.
- Hair must be fully pulled back and covered. Hat or hairnet, every shift.
- Hot food stays hot. Cold food stays cold. If you are unsure about a temperature, ask.
- If you drop it, it does not go back. No exceptions.
- Label and date everything you prepare or store.
- Know and follow the allergen protocols. The top 9 allergens, how to handle them, and what to do if a guest has a reaction are covered in your ServSafe Allergen Certification and in PB-002 Allergen Playbook. Never guess when an allergen is involved. Ask your manager.
- When in doubt, throw it out. Never serve food you are not sure about. Tell your manager.

Your site will have specific food safety protocols based on the account. Your manager will train you on those. The rules above apply everywhere, always.

# 06 How We Treat Each Other

The way we treat players, clients, and each other is as important as the food we serve.

> ANCHOR: Players, coaches, and staff notice how our team treats each other. A team that respects each other performs better and creates a better experience for everyone around them.

## With Players and Clients

You work in MLB clubhouses and player facilities. Players are the reason our clients hire us. They are entitled to their privacy and to being treated with complete professionalism. The Big Rules (AGR-001) govern how you interact with players and what you can and cannot share about what you see and hear at work. Read them. Sign them. Follow them.

- Do not photograph players, coaches, or the facility.
- Do not ask for autographs or personal contact information from players or coaches. Ever.
- Do not discuss what you see or hear at work on social media or with anyone outside the team.
- Be professional, warm, and helpful. That is our standard.

## Tips, Gratuity & Gifts

KitchFix employees do not accept individual tips, gratuities, or gifts. If a player, coach, or client staff member offers you something personally, thank them warmly and let your manager know. All gratuities go to your manager to be distributed to the team.

When a club chooses to provide a gratuity to the KitchFix team, it is distributed among hourly employees based on hours worked.

Gratuity from a club is not guaranteed and is not common across all accounts. Do not expect it, do not ask for it, and do not factor it into your financial planning.

## Social Media & Phones

Do not post photos, videos, or any content from client facilities on social media. This includes the kitchen, the dining room, players, coaches, and anything else you see at work. What happens in the clubhouse stays in the clubhouse.

Personal phone calls should be taken during breaks only. If you need to step away for an emergency, tell your manager.

## With Your Team

- Treat every teammate with respect - regardless of role, background, or how long they have been here.
- Harassment of any kind - verbal, physical, or through social media - is not tolerated and is grounds for termination.
- If someone on your team is treating you poorly, you have the right to report it. Talk to your manager, your Regional Director, or People Operations. See Section 07.

## Drugs & Alcohol

Do not come to work impaired. Do not consume alcohol or drugs on client property. Showing up under the influence or using alcohol or drugs during your shift are grounds for immediate termination.

## Referrals

Know someone who would be great on our team? Tell them to apply and let your manager know you referred them. KitchFix recognizes great referrals - ask your manager about the current referral program.

## Conduct That Can Get You Terminated Immediately

Some actions result in immediate termination with no prior warning:

- Theft - of anything belonging to the company, client, player, or a teammate.
- Fighting or physical altercation on client property.
- Showing up under the influence of drugs or alcohol, or consuming either on client property.
- Deliberate food safety or allergen violations.
- Harassment.
- Violating The Big Rules (AGR-001).

# 07 Your Rights & Protections

KitchFix is committed to a workplace that is fair, safe, and respectful. You have real rights here.

## Equal Treatment

KitchFix does not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other status protected by law. This applies to hiring, scheduling, pay, discipline, and every other aspect of your employment.

## Accommodations

If you need an adjustment to your work environment because of a disability or a sincerely held religious belief - whether it relates to your uniform, schedule, or duties - contact People Operations. We will work with you.

## Raising a Concern

If something at work does not feel right - how you are being treated, something you witnessed, a safety issue - you can and should raise it. Start with your manager. If you are not comfortable with that, go to your Regional Director or People Operations. The full process is in POL-001 Employee Concerns Policy.

You will not be punished for raising a concern in good faith. Retaliation against an employee who speaks up is a violation of KitchFix policy and is taken seriously.

## Workplace Injuries

If you are injured at work - no matter how minor it seems - tell your manager immediately. Do not wait until the end of your shift. KitchFix carries workers' compensation insurance and you are entitled to coverage for work-related injuries. You will not be penalized for reporting a workplace injury.

> NOTE: The full incident reporting and response process is in SOP-002 Safety & Incident Management. Your manager will walk you through what happens next.

## Progressive Discipline

If you have a performance or conduct issue, your manager will work through a documented process - coaching, warning, and escalation only if needed. You will know what the issue is, what you need to do differently, and what happens if it continues. The full process is in SOP-004 Formal Disciplinary Process.

> NOTE: A Spanish-language summary of POL-001 Employee Concerns Policy is available from People Operations upon request.

# 08 Pay & Your Schedule

What you need to know about how you get paid and how your schedule works.

## Pay

KitchFix pays hourly employees on a regular schedule. Your pay is processed through Rippling. You can view your pay stubs and update your direct deposit information in the Rippling app.

If you believe there is an error in your paycheck - hours are wrong, deductions are incorrect, or something does not add up - tell your manager the same day you notice it. Do not wait. Wage concerns that are not reported promptly are harder to resolve.

## Schedule

Your schedule is in Rippling. Your manager sets it. Shifts are assigned based on the needs of the account and your availability. If you need to request time off or a schedule change, speak with your manager as early as possible.

## Seasonal Employment

Most KitchFix hourly positions are seasonal and tied to the baseball calendar. Your employment begins and ends with the season unless otherwise agreed in writing. Returning for the next season is not guaranteed - it depends on your performance and the needs of the account.

> NOTE: Questions about pay, benefits eligibility, or your employment status go to People Operations: Mariela Chavez - through your manager or Regional Director.

# 09 Who to Call

You will have questions. Here is who can answer them.

| If you have a question about... | Talk to... |
|---|---|
| Your schedule or shift | Your site manager (Executive Chef) |
| Your paycheck or hours | Your site manager first, then People Operations (Mariela Chavez) |
| Your uniform or equipment | Your site manager |
| A safety concern | Your site manager immediately. If it is a life-safety emergency - call 911 first, then notify your manager. |
| A workplace injury | Tell your manager immediately. Do not wait. |
| A concern about how you are being treated | Your site manager, Regional Director, or People Operations. See POL-001. |
| Something that doesn't feel right | Tell your manager or Regional Director. You will not be penalized for asking. |

> NOTE: In any life-safety emergency - medical, fire, or physical threat - call 911 first. Then notify your manager immediately. Do not wait for permission to call 911.

When in doubt, ask your manager first. Most questions have a simple answer and your manager is the right first stop for almost everything. If they don't know, they will find out.
