# Purpose & Scope

This Standard governs how KitchFix employees communicate internally - which channels to use, how to use them, and what does not belong in writing.

> ANCHOR: How we communicate reflects who we are. Sloppy, careless, or inappropriate communication in any channel creates real consequences - for the team, the client relationship, and the company.

This Standard applies to all KitchFix employees. It governs internal communication across Slack, email, phone, text, the People Portal, and in-person conversations in a professional context. It does not govern communication with external clients or media - those are governed by AGR-001 The Big Rules.

This Standard is the third leg of the KitchFix documentation system alongside STD-001 (Documentation Format Standard) and STD-002 (Visual Communication Standard). Together they define how KitchFix produces and communicates information consistently.

# Channel Standards

Use the right channel for the right message. Channel discipline reduces noise, protects records, and keeps the team operating clearly.

| Channel | Use For | Not For | Response Expectation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slack | Fast coordination, quick questions, team updates, shift logistics | Sensitive HR matters, formal decisions, anything needing a record | Same day during working hours |
| Email | Formal communication, documentation, anything that needs a paper trail, external parties | Urgent operational issues - use Slack or call | 24-48 hours |
| Phone / Text | Urgent operational issues, emergencies, same-day coordination when Slack is insufficient | Formal decisions or anything that should be documented | Immediately for urgent. Same day otherwise. |
| In Person | Sensitive conversations (performance, discipline, personal concerns), decisions that need discussion | Routine updates that could be a Slack message | Scheduled in advance for formal conversations |
| People Portal | HR actions, PAFs, formal requests, policy documents, onboarding materials | Day-to-day team communication | Per process - People Ops will acknowledge |

> NOTE: Rippling is used for scheduling and payroll actions, not general communication. Google Drive is the repository for documents, not a communication channel. Neither replaces the channels above.

# Slack Standards

Slack is the primary operational communication channel for the KitchFix leadership team. It is fast, visible, and searchable - which means everything written there is a record.

> ANCHOR: Slack messages are corporate records. Write every message as if your manager, your client, or a lawyer could read it tomorrow. Because they could.

## Channel Usage

Use the correct channel for the topic. Do not post operational updates in HR channels or personnel matters in general channels.

Tag people when you need their attention. Do not tag the whole channel for individual requests.

Direct messages are appropriate for one-to-one coordination. Sensitive topics - performance, conduct, personal concerns - belong in person or in a documented channel, not DM.

Keep channels clean. Long threads on resolved topics should be archived, not left open indefinitely.

## Tone & Professionalism

Write clearly and professionally. Slack is not text messaging. Abbreviations, slang, and informal shorthand appropriate between friends are not appropriate in a work channel.

Do not use Slack to vent, complain about colleagues, or share frustration about clients or accounts. If you have a concern, raise it through the appropriate channel (POL-001).

Humor is welcome. Sarcasm that could be misread is not. When in doubt, default to direct and professional.

## What Does Not Belong on Slack

Personnel decisions, disciplinary actions, or performance feedback. These belong in person, documented through SOP-004.

Confidential HR information about any employee.

Client or player information covered by AGR-001.

Financial information, contract terms, or account-specific commercial information.

Anything you would not want screenshotted and shared outside the company.

# Email Standards

Email is the channel for formal communication, documentation, and anything that needs a paper trail.

## When to Use Email

Formal communications with People Operations, SLT, or external parties.

Anything that requires a documented record - approvals, decisions, policy notices.

Communications that will be referenced later or need to be forwarded.

Any communication with counsel, insurance carriers, or regulatory bodies.

## Email Discipline

Use your KitchFix email address (@kitchfix.com) for all work communication. Personal email accounts are not to be used for KitchFix business.

Subject lines should be clear and specific. "Question" is not a subject line. "Q3 STL account - linen vendor change" is.

Reply promptly. The expected response window for internal email is 24-48 hours during working days. If you cannot respond fully, acknowledge receipt and give a timeline.

Do not use email to escalate conflicts or create a paper trail designed to harm a colleague. If you have a concern, use POL-001.

> NOTE: KitchFix email is company property. It is subject to review by the company at any time. Do not use it for personal matters or for any communication you would not want the company to see.

# Sensitive Communications

Some conversations must not happen in writing. Others must.

## Have in Person - Document After

The following conversations happen verbally, in private, then are documented in writing through the appropriate form:

Performance coaching and disciplinary conversations (FORM-003, FORM-004 per SOP-004).

Termination conversations (FORM-006 per SOP-004).

Salary, compensation, or benefit discussions.

Concerns raised under POL-001.

Any conversation involving a specific employee's personal circumstances, medical situation, or family matter.

## Must Be in Writing

The following must be documented in writing, using the appropriate KitchFix form or channel:

All disciplinary actions - FORM-003 through FORM-006.

PAFs and payroll actions - People Portal.

Schedule changes and approved time off - Rippling.

Any decision or agreement that affects an employee's pay, status, or employment terms.

> NOTE: A verbal commitment that is not documented is not an official commitment. If you make an agreement with an employee about pay, scheduling, or any employment term, put it in writing through the appropriate channel the same day.

# Confidentiality

Internal communications are confidential. What is discussed inside KitchFix stays inside KitchFix.

Employees may not share internal communications - Slack messages, emails, documents, or verbal discussions - with external parties without authorization. This includes sharing screenshots, forwarding emails, or repeating conversations from internal channels.

AGR-001 The Big Rules governs confidentiality obligations specifically related to clients, players, and account information. This Standard governs internal operational communications. Both apply.

Violations of this Standard are subject to disciplinary action under SOP-004, up to and including termination.
