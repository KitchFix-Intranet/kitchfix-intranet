### B.2 Per-meal rates

> "**Major League Player Meals**. For Meals prepared and served by the Provider, in accordance with this SOW #1, to a Major League Player during a Major League Baseball season (including during the Major League Team Spring Training Period, and, if the Major League Team plays some or all of its regular season home games or postseason home games in Dunedin, Florida, during the Major League Team's regular season home games or postseason home games), the Club shall pay the Provider Twenty Dollars and Twenty Nine Cents (USD $20.29) per Meal, plus applicable taxes (the "**MLB Player Meal Fee**")."

`[Source: Complete_with_DocuSign_Final_Dunedin_Caterin - Final.pdf Schedule "A" § 12(b)(i) p.30]`

### B.4 Escalation clause

> "**Request to Increase Fees**. The Provider will be entitled to increase the price of each Meal Fee and/or the Service Fee on one (1) occasion only during each of the second, third and, if applicable, fourth, fifth, and sixth SOW #1 Agreement Years, subject to a maximum percentage increase equivalent to the percentage increase (if any) identified in the United States federal Consumer Price Index "Food Away From Home" category, as established by the Bureau of Labor Statistics calculated from the fourth quarter of the calendar year immediately preceding the applicable Agreement Year to the date upon which the Provider submits its price increase request. Notwithstanding the generality of the foregoing, no increase hereunder will be permitted unless:
> (i) each proposed price increase is necessitated solely, exclusively and directly as a result of a documented increase in the Provider's costs to obtain the ingredients or supplies necessary to provide the applicable Meal(s), Snacks and/or Shakes;
> (ii) the Supplier provides the Club with notice by January 31 prior to the applicable Agreement Year (the "**Price Increase Notice Deadline**") of each such proposed price increase along with (A) confirmation as to which Meal Fee(s) and/or Service Fee is subject to the price increase request, (B) evidence and documentation supporting the United States federal Consumer Price Index "Food Away From Home" category increase, and (C) evidence and documentation demonstrating compliance with paragraph 12(c)(i), above;
> (iii) the Provider is, at the time the price increase is proposed and thereafter, in compliance with this paragraph 12(c)(i), above, and
> (iv) after reviewing the proposed price increase and the supporting evidence and documentation, the Club provides the Provider with written approval of same, which approval will not be unreasonably withheld.
> In addition, for the second, third and, if applicable, fourth, fifth and sixth SOW #1 Agreement Years, to the extent that there is a *decrease* identified in the United States federal Consumer Price Index "Food Away From Home" category relating to any of the Meals, Snacks or Shakes prior to the Price Increase Notice Deadline, the impacted Meal Fees and Service Fee shall be accordingly deemed decreased for the subsequent Agreement Year."

`[Source: Complete_with_DocuSign_Final_Dunedin_Caterin - Final.pdf Schedule "A" § 12(c) pp.30-31]`
