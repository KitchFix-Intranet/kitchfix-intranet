## Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement under the following conditions:

- **Termination for Convenience.** After completion of the initial three-year term, either party may terminate with one hundred twenty (120) days written notice, with the termination effective at the end of the then-current MLB regular season to avoid mid-season disruption.
- **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate for material breach upon thirty (30) days written notice, provided that the breaching party has been given a thirty (30) day cure period and has failed to cure the breach.
- **Material Breach Defined.** Material breach includes, without limitation: failure to deliver services as defined in this Agreement, failure to maintain required certifications or insurance coverage, failure to comply with confidentiality obligations, failure to pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of due date, MLB league or governing-body sanction, or repeated documented operational failures not remediated through the corrective action process described in Section 7.
- **Transition Obligations.** Upon termination, both parties commit to a sixty (60) day transition period. KitchFix retains responsibility for service delivery through the effective termination date and provides reasonable cooperation for transition to a successor provider, including handoff of operating documents, equipment inventory, recipe library, vendor contacts, and pre-packaged product par lists.

> NOTE: Legal Review - Cure periods, indemnification, and limitation of liability cannot be drafted by the field. Section 1 in full requires legal review by John before execution.

## Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for failure to perform obligations under this Agreement if performance is prevented by an event beyond reasonable control, including: natural disaster, severe winter weather event, public health emergency, government shutdown, MLB league suspension of operations, fire, or extended utility failure.

During a force majeure event, the affected party notifies the other party within forty-eight (48) hours of the event. Service continuation, suspension, and any pricing adjustment are negotiated within seven (7) days of the event onset. Cold-weather and severe storm protocols specific to Montana operations are documented in Section 9. Suspension of MLB league play (game cancellations, lockouts, league-wide work stoppages) triggers separate provisions documented below.

**MLB league suspension of play.** If the MLB suspends regular-season operations for more than seven (7) consecutive calendar days, parties enter a service-suspension provision: home and visitor clubhouse service suspends for the duration, road food coordination suspends, fixed monthly fee continues at fifty percent (50%) for up to thirty (30) days of suspension, and resumes at one hundred percent (100%) on the day MLB play resumes. Suspensions exceeding thirty (30) days are renegotiated.

## KitchFix Provides

### Services Delivered

- Full home clubhouse food service for all 81 home regular-season games, exhibition games, post-season games, and work-out days at <NonCanonical>Big Sky Field</NonCanonical>.
- Full visitor clubhouse food service for all 81 home regular-season games and any post-season home games. Visitor clubhouse menus are coordinated with Client's Visitor Clubhouse Manager and follow KitchFix performance nutrition standards adapted to visiting clubs.
- Road food coordination for all 81 away regular-season games and any away post-season games - vendor sourcing, ordering, delivery coordination, dietary-restriction accommodation, and on-the-ground troubleshooting.

- Menu development on the series-based cadence defined in Section 4.
- Made-to-order service from clubhouse open through the beginning of the third inning of each game.
- Daily sanitation, cleaning, and back-of-house facility upkeep within the kitchen and dining areas of both clubhouses.
- Inventory management, food cost tracking, and pre-packaged product par list maintenance.
- Compliance with KitchFix Level SOPs in full, including the Allergen Playbook, ServSafe standards, menu approval workflow, and communication conventions.

### Points of Service - Home Clubhouse

- Main dining hall - buffet line, salad bar, deli station, breads and spreads, oatmeal bar, made-to-order station, snackie/appetizer station, pizza station, action stations.
- Grab-and-go cooler - stocked at all times during clubhouse operating hours.
- Pre-packaged product display - stocked from a collaboratively-maintained par list.
- Beverage program - coffee bar, juice bar, fountain station, milk and milk alternatives.
- Coaches' lounge service - coffee bar, beverages, snack rotation.

### Points of Service - Visitor Clubhouse

- Buffet line scaled to visitor roster - typically 30-40 covers per meal.
- Grab-and-go and pre-packaged product display.
- Beverage program - coffee bar, fountain station, milk and milk alternatives.
- Made-to-order service for visitor clubhouse is limited to omelet station at breakfast and one rotating handheld at lunch - full made-to-order menu does not apply.

### Staffing - Regular Season

Home clubhouse - three (3) salaried positions and twelve (12) hourly positions during a standard homestand. Visitor clubhouse - one (1) salaried position and four (4) hourly positions during a standard homestand. Total operating roster of twenty (20) on a homestand day:

- Home Executive Chef (salaried) - site leader, full operational ownership, primary point of contact for Home Clubhouse Manager.
- Home Sous Chef (salaried) - kitchen execution and second-in-command, leads made-to-order and pizza stations.
- Home Hospitality Manager (salaried) - client-facing, FOH, AP/AR, scheduling, dining operations.
- Visitor Sous Chef (salaried) - visitor clubhouse leadership, primary point of contact for Visitor Clubhouse Manager.
- Latin Lead Cook (hourly, designated) - Latin program execution, cross-trained on home and visitor clubhouses. Sourced specifically for Latin culinary specialty.
- Cooks (hourly) - line, prep, and station execution.
- FOH Attendants (hourly) - service line, dining hall, beverage program, grab-and-go restock.
- Dishwashers (hourly) - back-of-house sanitation.

### Staffing - Off-Days, Travel Days, and Off-Season

Off-days - reduced service per the Service Calendar (Section 3). Approximately five (5) staff cover the operation. Travel days where the home team is on the road but visitor clubhouse remains active for incoming series - visitor clubhouse staffing remains at five (5); home clubhouse operates a reduced grab-and-go and beverage program. Off-season - facility closed except for scheduled work-out days, which are staffed at three (3) for grab-and-go service.

### Road Food Coordination

- KitchFix sources and orders food for all 81 away games through a vetted national vendor network.
- Menu submitted by KitchFix Road Food Coordinator to the part-time RD and Visitor Clubhouse Manager (away city) one week before each road series.
- Dietary-restriction accommodation handled in advance through coordination with the part-time RD and the away clubhouse manager.
- Delivery timing coordinated with the away city clubhouse manager and KitchFix-side dispatch.
- On-the-ground troubleshooting available by phone during all road meal periods.

## Schedule Changes

- Routine schedule changes (game-time shifts, weather makeups, doubleheaders, rainout reschedules, MLB-mandated time changes) are communicated by the Home Clubhouse Manager to KitchFix Hospitality Manager as soon as confirmed.

- Rainout post-game service is held to the standard Post-Game Buffet level - KitchFix does not stand down a Post-Game service unless the Home Clubhouse Manager directs.
- Schedule changes that exceed the standard service calendar (e.g. added work-out days beyond plan, special events) require RDO approval and may trigger pricing adjustment under Section 10.

## Menu Development and Approval

### Approval Workflow

Every series menu requires approval from the following parties before service:

<NonCanonical>

- Site Leader (Home Executive Chef) - operational feasibility, kitchen capacity, ingredient sourcing.
- KitchFix Director of Culinary - culinary standard, recipe quality, alignment with KitchFix culinary baseline.
- KitchFix part-time RD - nutrition philosophy, performance objectives, athlete specifications.
- Sasquatches MLB Performance Director (or designee) - final team-side approval.
- Home Clubhouse Manager - receives the approved menu and confirms service times.

</NonCanonical>

### Series-Based Menu Cycle

- **Cycle.** Series-based - every home series carries its own approved menu document.
- **Format.** Single PDF per series, with one tab per game day named by date (e.g. Saturday, 4/12).
- **Submission.** Submitted by email to the <NonCanonical>Sasquatches MLB Performance Director and Home Clubhouse Manager</NonCanonical> no fewer than seven (7) days before the start of the series.
- **Revision turnaround.** Performance Director provides feedback within four (4) days of the start of the series.
- **Final lock.** Final revision sent within forty-eight (48) hours of the start of the series.
- **Pre-series and post-series meetings.** KitchFix recommends and Client agrees to standing pre-series and post-series meetings - typically thirty (30) minutes each, scheduled between the Home Executive Chef, RD, Performance Director, and Home Clubhouse Manager.
- **Mid-series changes.** Any material deviation from an approved menu requires Performance Director and Home Clubhouse Manager notification before service. Substitutions for vendor unavailability, Client request, or commodity-price spike are documented and communicated same-day.

### Visitor Clubhouse Menu Cadence

Visitor menus are submitted to the Visitor Clubhouse Manager (incoming club) seventy-two (72) hours before the series starts. Visitor menus follow KitchFix performance nutrition standards and accommodate visitor club requests where reasonable.

### Account-Specific Menu Best Practices

<NonCanonical>

- Authentic Latin cuisine - Puerto Rican, Dominican, Venezuelan, and Cuban - featured every meal period. Non-negotiable. Latin Lead Cook role exists specifically to execute this standard.
- Menus highlight food holidays celebrated during the season; special menus prepared for any holiday that overlaps a home series.
- Lean cuts of protein at all meal periods. Heavy proteins reserved primarily for post-game.
- High-end proteins (beef tenderloin, ribeye, striploin; sea bass, halibut, red snapper, grouper) reserved primarily for post-game and used less frequently.
- All meals to include gluten-free and dairy-free options. Individually prepared meals to accommodate any documented dietary restriction.
- No frozen, breaded, pre-prepared products on any line.
- Pizza dough made from scratch in-house. Gluten-free pizza dough pre-purchased for cross-contamination control.
- French fries available upon request only - not a standing menu item.

</NonCanonical>

## Change Management

Operational changes - standing menu changes, service-time changes, station additions, format changes - follow this process:

- **Request.** Documented in writing by the requesting party (Client or KitchFix). Verbal requests are followed by same-day written confirmation.
