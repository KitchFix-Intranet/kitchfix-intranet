## Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement under the following conditions:

- **Termination for Convenience.** After completion of the initial three-year term, either party may terminate with sixty (60) days written notice.
- **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate for material breach upon thirty (30) days written notice, provided that the breaching party has been given a thirty (30) day cure period and has failed to cure the breach.
- **Material Breach Defined.** Material breach includes, without limitation: failure to deliver services as defined in this Agreement, failure to maintain required certifications or insurance coverage, failure to comply with confidentiality obligations, failure to pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of due date, or repeated documented operational failures not remediated through the corrective action process described in Section 7.
- **Transition Obligations.** Upon termination, both parties commit to a thirty (30) day transition period. KitchFix retains responsibility for service delivery through the effective termination date and provides reasonable cooperation for transition to a successor provider, including handoff of operating documents, equipment inventory, and vendor contacts.

> NOTE: Legal Review - Cure periods, indemnification, and limitation of liability cannot be drafted by the field. Section 1 in full requires legal review by John before execution.

## Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for failure to perform obligations under this Agreement if performance is prevented by an event beyond reasonable control, including: natural disaster, hurricane, public health emergency, government shutdown, league suspension of operations, fire, or extended utility failure.

During a force majeure event, the affected party notifies the other party within forty-eight (48) hours of the event. Service continuation, suspension, and any pricing adjustment are negotiated within seven (7) days of the event onset. Hurricane preparedness and post-storm reentry protocols specific to Florida operations are documented in Section 9.

## Menu Development and Approval

### Approval Workflow

Every menu cycle requires approval from four parties before service:

<NonCanonical>

- Site Leader (Executive Chef) - operational feasibility, kitchen capacity, ingredient sourcing.
- KitchFix Director of Culinary - culinary standard, recipe quality, alignment with KitchFix culinary baseline.
- Sea Slugs MLB Dietitian - nutrition philosophy, performance objectives, athlete specifications.
- Sea Slugs MiLB Coordinator - MiLB roster considerations, feedback from prior cycles.

</NonCanonical>

### Menu Cycle and Lead Time

- Cycle length. Four (4) week rotating cycle, refreshed seasonally to align with FCL, Extended ST, Spring Training, and Instructional League calendars.
- Submission. <NonCanonical>Draft menu submitted to the Sea Slugs Dietitian no fewer than two (2) weeks before service start.</NonCanonical>
- Revision turnaround. <NonCanonical>Sea Slugs Dietitian provides feedback within seven (7) days of receipt.</NonCanonical>
- Approval lock. Final menu signed off no fewer than seven (7) days before service start.
- Mid-cycle changes. Any material deviation from an approved menu requires Dietitian notification before service. Substitutions for product quality or availability are documented and communicated same-day.

### Account-Specific Menu Best Practices

<NonCanonical>

- Latin cuisine is featured every meal period - non-negotiable.
- Two protein options at every lunch and dinner, with at least one lean option (e.g. grilled chicken breast, white-fleshed fish, lean beef cut).
- Two carbohydrate options at lunch and dinner - at least one whole grain or starchy vegetable.
- At least one non-starchy vegetable at lunch and dinner, prepared with minimal added oil.
- No two same proteins served on the same day.
- Soup rotation - daily, broth-based preferred.
- Composed salads - one per day, rotated across tuna, chicken, salmon, egg, and seasonal pasta options. Avocado mayo or EVOO base - no full-fat mayo bases.

</NonCanonical>

## Change Management

Operational changes - standing menu changes, service-time changes, station additions, format changes - follow this process:

- **Request.** Documented in writing by the requesting party (Client or KitchFix). Verbal requests are followed by same-day written confirmation.
- **Evaluation.** Site Leader and RDO evaluate operational and financial impact within seven (7) days.
- **Approval.** Material changes require Director of Culinary, Dietitian, and RDO sign-off. Pricing-impact changes require VPO sign-off.
- **Documentation.** Approved changes are logged in the Account Change Log. The SLA is updated at the next ABR to reflect changes that have become standard practice.
- **Scope creep prevention.** Small changes accumulate. The annual ABR review explicitly reconciles the SLA against actual practice. If the field operates differently than the SLA describes, either the field corrects or the SLA is updated.

# 06 Section 5 - Nutrition Guidelines and Allergen Standards

Nutrition philosophy and guidelines, portion standards, allergen handling, and emergency response protocols. The KitchFix Allergen Playbook is the company-wide standard; account-level overrides are documented here.

## Nutrition Guidelines

<NonCanonical>Sea Slugs Nutrition Operating Guidelines, authored by the Sea Slugs Performance Nutrition staff,</NonCanonical> govern menu philosophy at this account. The Operating Guidelines are attached to this Agreement as a referenced document and updated annually at the ABR. Where the Operating Guidelines are silent, the KitchFix culinary baseline applies.

## Performance Nutrition Framework

- **Fuel performance.** Meals before, during, and after fieldwork are nutrient-dense and performance-aligned.
- **Improve recovery.** Anti-inflammatory foods, lean proteins, complex carbohydrates, and colorful produce are prioritized.
- **Maintain healthy playing weight.** Portion standards, lean protein emphasis, and minimal added fats support roster weight management.
- **Aid in immune health.** Variety, color, and seasonal produce are featured at every meal.
- **Player satisfaction.** Food is enjoyable. Variety, flavor, and presentation are non-negotiable.

## Cooking Methods

- Approved: grilling, baking, roasting, broiling, pan-searing with minimal oil.
- Restricted: frying, par-frying, breading. Pre-breaded items are not permitted. Freshly breaded and baked items are permitted with prior Dietitian approval.
- Approved fats and oils: avocado oil (preferred), extra virgin olive oil, grapeseed oil, sesame oil, grass-fed butter (sparingly).
- Restricted: high saturated fat preparations, gravies, heavy cream sauces, cheese-laden sauces.

## Foods to Limit

<NonCanonical>

- Meat on the bone - permitted in appropriate Latin settings; labeled and called out.
- Pork bacon - permitted at MLB camp daily, MiLB three (3) times per week during Spring Training and twice per week during the rest of the season.
- Refined carbohydrates - white rice acceptable within rotation; white pasta and white bread limited to specific applications.
- Foods that may cause digestive discomfort before games (e.g. cruciferous vegetables, chili).

</NonCanonical>

## Foods to Avoid

- Candy, cookies, doughnuts, and other high-sugar / low-nutrient foods.
- Highly processed foods - hot dogs, low-quality lunch meats, processed cheese alternatives, cheese product spreads.
- Nutritionally empty foods - iceberg lettuce, sugared cereals.

## Allergen Position

The KitchFix Allergen Playbook applies in full at <NonCanonical>the Sea Slugs PDC</NonCanonical>. The Playbook is the company-wide standard and is not modified to accommodate this account.

### Standing Positions from the Allergen Playbook

- **Top 9 allergens.** Milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans, and sesame are tracked and labeled at every meal period.
- **Labeling.** Every hot and cold line item carries a label in English and Spanish, listing the dish name and applicable Top 9 allergens.
- **No allergen-free meals or stations.** KitchFix does not represent any meal or station as allergen-free. Severe allergy accommodation is handled case by case at the Chef level through the Dietitian - not by station designation.
- **Peanut and Tree Nut Standard.** No peanuts or tree nuts on the open buffet. Limited individually-packaged exceptions per the Playbook (e.g. PB&J, individual nut butter cups, nut-topped granola) are permitted with clear labeling.
- **Vendor substitution protocol.** Per the Playbook, substitutions are evaluated against the original product's allergen profile before service. Substitutions that introduce a new Top 9 allergen require Dietitian notification before service.
- **Allergen storage protocol.** Per the Playbook - segregated storage, dedicated utensils, color-coded handling for high-risk preparations.

### Account-Level Allergen Overrides

<NonCanonical>No account-level overrides apply at the Sea Slugs PDC.</NonCanonical> The Allergen Playbook applies in full.

## Severe Allergy and Emergency Response

<NonCanonical>

- **Severe allergy escalation.** Players or staff with severe allergies are flagged to KitchFix in advance of camp arrival by the Sea Slugs Dietitian. Accommodation plans are built per individual through the Chef and Dietitian.
- **Anaphylaxis response.** Sea Slugs medical staff (athletic trainer or team physician) is the primary responder. KitchFix Site Leader is notified immediately by FOH staff, food preparation in the affected area is paused, and 911 is contacted by Sea Slugs medical staff. KitchFix supports the investigation by preserving food samples, ingredient lists, and prep records as requested.
- **Foodborne illness investigation.** If foodborne illness is suspected, KitchFix preserves the affected food and ingredient samples for forty-eight (48) hours and cooperates with Sea Slugs medical staff and the Florida Department of Health. Notification chain: Site Leader to RDO to VPO to Sea Slugs Director of Player Development Operations.
- **Emergency contacts.** Documented in the site Emergency Reference posted in the kitchen. Updated annually at the Pre-ST Review and at any change in personnel.

</NonCanonical>
