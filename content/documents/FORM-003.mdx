FORM-003 - v1.0 - KitchFix Internal

> NOTE: This form documents a coaching conversation or verbal warning. It is the manager's record only - the employee does not sign. File in the site employee folder and submit to People Operations within 24 hours.

# Employee & Incident Information

- EMPLOYEE NAME
- ACCOUNT / SITE
- ROLE / POSITION
- DATE OF CONVERSATION
- MANAGER NAME
- RDO NOTIFIED (NAME & DATE)

# Topic & Standard

WHAT IS THE SPECIFIC BEHAVIOR observed OR PERFORMANCE ISSUE?

WHAT IS THE EXPECTED behavior or STANDARD?

# Prior History

Has this issue been previously discussed with the employee (verbally, informally)?

- No - first time this issue has been raised.
- Yes - previously discussed informally on: ___________________________ Add the name of the manager that had these conversations with the employee if different from manager having current conversation.

# Outcome & Agreed Next Steps

WHAT WAS AGREED IN THE CONVERSATION? WHAT DOES THE EMPLOYEE NEED TO DO DIFFERENTLY?

FOLLOW-UP DATE / CHECK-IN (IF APPLICABLE)

# Manager Signature

I confirm that the above conversation took place as documented.

Manager Signature

Date

> NOTE: Employee does NOT sign this form. This is the manager's record of the coaching conversation. If the employee makes a written response they wish to have filed, attach it to this form.
