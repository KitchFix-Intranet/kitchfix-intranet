## Plain-english summary

1. **The build in one sentence:** when a site leader finalizes their week in
   the Service Calendar, the intranet turns those counts into a draft invoice
   sitting in QuickBooks for Sebastian to check and send, and notifies by
   email.
2. **Why it matters:** Sebastian's copy-and-retype routine shipped a wrong
   invoice on camera during his own demo. The export deletes the step where
   errors happen, and gives the company one source of truth for billing
   numbers instead of three.
3. **Why this project exists now:** the meeting happened, the process is
   mapped, QBO access is live and surveyed, the SC data model (sc-1..29) is
   finished, and Kevin's core rulings landed 2026-08-06.
4. **Rough size:** three build PRs plus one or two small migrations, then 2-3
   shadow weeks of validation. The calendar time is mostly validation, not
   code.

## 6. Architectural seeds (constraints for the Phase 2 spec, decided by
evidence and rulings in hand)

- **The export ledger is the future lock signal.** One row per generated
  invoice: account, week, payload hash, QBO invoice id, DocNumber, status.
  When sc-25's period lock swaps its input from "date passed" to "AP has
  pulled," THIS table is what it reads. Design it once, correctly.
- **One money path, actuals only.** The builder reads the same
  effective-dated `sc_daily_revenue` totals every other surface reads -
  actual rows, never projections (R2; C-11 showed projections miss real
  invoices by +12% to +50%). Nothing recomputes sticker x count. (R8.)
- **Week from `service_date` only.** `week_label` is period-relative and
  banned from billing-week logic (C-3/C-12 trap).
- **Per-day lines, prices included (K-2).** The payload mirrors the real
  anatomy: one line per service per day, meal breakdown in the description,
  Sunday TxnDate, per-venue TaxCodeRef, SC as the rate truth.
- **Finalize = push + notify (K-1).** The site leader's finalize action
  creates the QBO draft and fires the styled notification email in the same
  motion. Failure of the push never silently eats the finalize - retry and
  error surfacing are spec sections.
- **Billed weeks are frozen (K-3).** Server-enforced. Post-bill corrections
  go through Sebastian manually in phase 1; adjustment-line automation is a
  post-proof revisit.
- **QBO assigns DocNumbers.** The adapter never fights the K3 sequence.
- **Drafts only.** The adapter creates; only Sebastian sends. No code path
  to QBO's send operation exists in v1.
- **Split rules are per-account config**, not code branches - the Rogers
  four-invoices-per-week pattern makes this table-shaped from day one.
- **Structural exclusions:** fee-schedule money, `is_non_revenue` items, fee
  accounts, and TXR - TX - V (removed per K-5) never enter the builder.
  Unfinalized weeks are unbillable at the API, not just hidden in the UI.
