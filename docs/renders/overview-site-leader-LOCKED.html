What survives, and why

What is left. $18,981 for 7 days, $2,712 a day, against the $2,258 a day averaged so far. The only figure on the page that converts to a Tuesday decision.

Pace as a sentence, not a bar - spending slower or faster than the clock, with both percentages behind it.

A note on the percentages. COGS% and margin% are to-date over to-date. While revenue is planned, revenue-to-date equals budget-to-date, so the percentage is the dollar variance rescaled - not a second signal. Once Service Calendar counts are live the denominators separate and the percentage starts earning its place.
