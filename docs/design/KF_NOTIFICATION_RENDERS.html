KitchFix Ops Hub · Service Calendar · Billing

Notification renders

Every message the billing arc sends, as it will arrive. Content and recipients follow the signed spec §9. Nothing here is built to send yet - N1 and N2 compose but are fenced; N3 and N4 are not built. Review the words as carefully as the layout: these land in AP's inbox on a Tuesday morning and in a site leader's phone on a Friday.

Note for the build: these are design previews. Production email needs table-based, inline-styled markup to survive Outlook and Gmail; the content, hierarchy, and copy carry over exactly, the CSS does not.
