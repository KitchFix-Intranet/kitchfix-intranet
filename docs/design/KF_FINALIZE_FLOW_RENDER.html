The month-boundary fix

Option A, as ruled. A week is always the whole Mon-Sun week, even when the month cuts through it. The days that belong to the other month render ghosted but are counted, and the same week appears in both months carrying the same total. One week, one button, one truth - the month is a viewing frame, not a billing boundary.
