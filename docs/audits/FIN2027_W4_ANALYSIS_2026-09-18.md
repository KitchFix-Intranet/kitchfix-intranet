**Top-4 drivers (91% of same-store decline)**: TBR-FL, STL-MO, TXR-AZ, CIN-OH. **None carries a qualifying Stage 3 bias.** Three different drivers: food inflation (TBR-FL); fee-account labor already banked in FY26 (STL-MO, CIN-OH); volume drop (TXR-AZ, where FY27 revenue budget sits below FY25 actual).

**Portfolio food-budget growth vs revenue-budget growth**: 5 of 8 accounts show food up 10-29% against revenue up 3-6%. Deliberate margin compression priced into FY27 plan.

**Absorption test per qualifying line**: residual = Stage 3 bias − Joe's FY26→FY27 step. Most TBJ-FL biases partially or over-absorbed. CIN-AZ 2400.1 (revenue LIGHT): Joe moved budget UP against a LIGHT bias, widening exposure. Cleanest actionable flag in the analysis.

**Fee vs per-meal split**: 4 fee accounts (CIN-OH, STL-MO, STL-FL, TXR-TX-H), 6 per-meal accounts, 1 standalone (TXR-TX-V). Fee compression is a contract question; per-meal compression is a cost management question. Different slides, different recommendations.

## Stage 5 · Lockout exposure

**Method**: Full MLB vs No MLB per-period plan-band; bookends only (fixed-cost lines don't step down in partial-year lockout without Joe's PR-6 rule).

**Corrections applied**:
- STL-FL removed from cumulative curve (fee is contract-fixed identical in both scenarios; per-period variance is pure timing, not exposure).
- Curve relabeled "cumulative net revenue difference" (not "at risk"); non-monotonic behavior was a logic problem in the "at risk" framing.
- CM at risk expressed as a range (as-modeled upper bound; if fee-account salary → 0 with revenue, lower bound).

**Portfolio CM at risk (as a band)**:
- As modeled: upper bound (salary held at Full MLB levels)
- If fee-account salary drops to zero with revenue: lower bound (add-back of 3 fee-account salaries)
- Ex-TXR-TX-V variant also published

**Curve shape**: majority of exposure concentrated in Spring Training P1-P3. A lockout resolved before ST costs a fraction of one resolved after. That is the decision-relevant fact.

**MiLB-immune (0 delta both scenarios)**: CIN-KY, TBJ-NY.

**STL-FL paradox**: Full-year CM is HIGHER under No MLB (contract fee holds, hourly labor cut). Contract-adjustment clause caveat noted.

**PR-6 gap**: Joe's model has no cost-step-down rule for a partial-year lockout. Mid-season CM point estimate not derivable from current model. Bookends and ST step are the honest publishable numbers.
