Accent - contrast-split

Amber is contrast-split because white text on amber-500 fails AA (3.19). Use --accent-solid when the label must be white, --accent-text when amber IS the text.

--accent-solid
amber-600 · white text OK
--accent-text on --accent-backplate
amber-700 · CTA wash
--accent-text on surface
