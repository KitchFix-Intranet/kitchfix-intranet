## Q5 - What each dimension means for a board

### At-risk (CIN-AZ, CIN-KY, TBJ-FL, TBJ-NY, TBR-FL, TXR-AZ, TXR-TX-H, TXR-TX-V)
- **State pills:** over/under state pills apply. Red for over.
- **Pace variance:** the current design (percentage variance vs budget, capped ±100% with a clipped indicator per PLAYBOOK §7) is appropriate.
- **Bonused-on rendering:** Total COGS − labor − salary − food − packaging − vehicle (D15b) drives the operator KPI; render at the four-group level (3100 / 3200 / 3400 / 3500) per PLAYBOOK §8.2 to avoid the salary-subtraction leak.

### Cost-reimbursable pass-through (CIN-OH, STL-FL, STL-MO)
- **What replaces the state pill:** UNKNOWN - not derivable from PG data we hold today. The KPI-suppression signal from BRIEF ("operator is not held to it as a KPI") means the current at-risk board renderers would show a false verdict. **Recommendation to Kevin:** either (a) new label like "Billed back to client - not a KitchFix P&L exposure" per line, OR (b) suppress the state pill entirely on `13xx` reimbursable lines at these three accounts.
- **Is "billed back to client" derivable from data we hold today?** Partially - reimbursables `13xx` line codes are already routed distinct from COGS `32xx/34xx/35xx` per PLAYBOOK §5.2. What is NOT held today: the client-invoice-back timestamp/amount (i.e. was it actually billed to the client this period? when?). That's a data gap - the reimbursable spend is captured; the client-reimbursement bill-back event is not.
- **Does the three-bucket rollup apply?** The three-bucket rollup (labor / food / packaging or supplies / vehicle) is a KitchFix P&L construct; at pass-through accounts the food+packaging+supplies portion is billed back. Rendering the three-bucket rollup unchanged would double-count from an economic perspective (it shows KitchFix as bearing cost that it doesn't). Recommendation: at pass-through accounts, the `13xx` bucket must be rendered as a separate "billed back" line, and food/supplies (`3200/3400`) if any should be suppressed or rendered as $0 to reflect economic reality. **Kevin rules whether to net or gross-up.**

### Revenue-flex envelope (TXR-TX-V labor only)
- **How the envelope surfaces:** labor budget = revenue × `accounts.labor_ratio` (0.1923). Rendered against **adjusted envelope**, not original budget, per PLAYBOOK §7 (Variance display rules) and BUSINESS_NOTES §137-151.
- **Behavior before revenue is known:** the tile is a funnel: quoted, booked, delivered, invoiced, collected. Per PLAYBOOK §4.6: "annual budget vs booked YTD as the closer." Before revenue is known, the tile shows the FORECAST envelope ($312,000/yr) and pending status.
- **Purchasing rollup:** TXR-TX-V is **excluded from aggregate purchasing rollups** per code (`V6_ENVELOPE_ACCOUNTS` at PURCH_ROUTE:84,213). Any board summing purchasing across accounts must skip TXR-TX-V or show it as a separate line `[code-read src/app/api/kpi/purchasing/route.js:213]`.

### Flat-fee for purchasing specifically
- **Purchasing tile at a flat-fee account:** the SF is fixed and non-varying, so purchasing variance is what the operator controls. At the two at-risk flat-fees (TXR-TX-H, TXR-TX-V), the operator IS on the hook for purchasing overruns because ingredients/supplies are baked into the fee (per TXR-TX-H §2c). At the three pass-through flat-fees (CIN-OH, STL-FL, STL-MO), the operator is NOT on the hook for the reimbursable stream (see cost-reimbursable section above). **So `billing_model=flat_fee` is not a sufficient signal for the purchasing render** - the board must additionally know the cost-model dimension, which the data does not carry today.
