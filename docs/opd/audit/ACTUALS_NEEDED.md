## 7. Why this register matters

Per Charter §9 and Audit Brief §2: a confidently-wrong document becomes a confidently-wrong brain. Loading any of the Tier 1 docs with unfilled actuals into the SousAI corpus would produce Sous answers that cite the doc by name and section while quoting unverified content. Hard floor rule 2 (zero tolerance on numbers) and rule 1 (never invent) protect against the worst cases but the chunk still ranks and the citation still lands.

Kevin's role at CK-4 is to supply the actuals or to confirm the deferral. The audit's role is to surface the gap precisely - which it has.
