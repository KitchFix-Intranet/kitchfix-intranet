## §B. Operative terms — VERBATIM

### B.1 Term / duration

> "A. Term. This Agreement shall commence on January 1, 2023 and shall expire on December 31, 2025 (the "Initial Term"), unless sooner terminated as provided herein. B. Renewal Term. The Club shall have the option to extend the Initial Term of this Agreement for two (2) additional, consecutive twelve-month periods commencing on January 1, 2026 and January 1, 2027, respectively (each additional twelve-month period is referred to as a "Renewal Term❞ and together with the Initial Term, the "Term"). If the Club desires to exercise its option to extend the Initial Term the Club will provide written notice to Kitchfix on or before November 1, 2025. Similarly, the Club desires to exercise its option for the second Renewal Term, the Club will provide written notice to Kitchfix on or before November 1, 2026. If the Club does not provide notice to Kitchfix to extend the Term in accordance with this Section I(B)this Agreement will expire at 5:00 p.m. (local time) on December 31st of the applicable year."

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § I.A-B p.1]

### B.2 Per-meal rates

> "In addition to the Service Fee, the Club agrees to pay to Kitchfix the following fees (the "Catering Fees") for the Catering Services in 2023:
> 1. Major League Catering Services Pricing (during Major League Spring Training only)
> a) Breakfast - $17.88 per person
> b) Lunch - $17.88 per person
> c) Dinner - $17.88 per person
> 2. Minor League Catering Services Pricing
> a) Breakfast - $11.35 per person
> b) Lunch - $11.35 per person
> c) Dinner - $11.35 per person
> d) Snack - $4.51 per person
> e) 3. Club will have the option to add a Late Night meal that will be priced: i) $12.77 per person if the Club only serves two (2) hot meals in a day which are more than eight (8) hours apart; or ii) at the same cost as the Dinner per person rate in all other cases."

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § IV.B.1-3 p.6]

Exhibit B tiered pricing (post-meal-count threshold):

> "Meals will be billed at the following rates once a total of 72,890 meals have been billed in 2023:
> O Minor League Catering Services Pricing
> Breakfast - $16.22 per person
> Lunch - $16.22 per person
> Dinner - $16.22 per person
> Snack - $6.44 per person"

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § Exhibit B p.14]

Beverage service option:

> "4. Each year of the Term, the Club will have the option to add coffee service at the rate of $450/week and fountain beverages at the rate of $250/week. No taxes will be assessed and/or collected in connection with the beverage service. If the Club exercises either options for beverage services, Kitchfix agrees that the fee shall be calculated based on a maximum of 45 weeks of beverage service in the applicable calendar year."

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § IV.B.4 pp.6-7]

Educational services option:

> "C. In the event that the Club requests Kitchfix to provide "educational services" related to food preparation, Kitchfix will charge $1,000.00 for each cooking class/cooking demonstration plus any applicable taxes. The parties shall mutually agree on details related to the educational services and individual cooking classes."

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § IV.C p.7]

### B.3 Service fee

> "A. The Club will pay to Kitchfix the "Service Fee" calculated and described in Exhibit B.
> 1. The Service Fee will be payable as follows:
> a) 75% of the Service Fee is due and payable on February 1st each year of the Term; and
> b) The remaining 25% of the Service Fee is due and payable on March 15th each year of the Term.
> 2. Kitchfix shall provide the Club with an invoice for the Service Fee no later than January 15th each year of the Term."

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § IV.A p.6]

Exhibit B — Service Fee calculation:

> "The Service Fee is calculated as follows:
> Kitchfix and Club are estimating a budget, based on the projected number of total meals and inclusive of sales tax assuming a 10.3% rate for Arizona, of $1,478,082 for the year. This budget results in $1,340,056 of costs, before taxes
> The Service Fee is calculated as 30% of the pre-tax budget estimate and equal to $402,016.
> The pricing reflected in section IV is set to combine with the Service Fee to reach these budget figures"

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § Exhibit B p.14]

### B.4 Escalation clause

> "3. Beginning with calendar year 2024 and each year of the Term thereafter,, the per meal cost will be determined by the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U): U.S. City Average, Food Away from Home annual increase as of October. Annual rate increases shall have a floor of 2% and a cap of 5%."

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § IV.B.3 p.6]

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § I.F p.3]

### B.7 Passthrough / reimbursement

NOT PRESENT as an explicit food-cost passthrough. The AZ agreement uses a fixed Service Fee + per-meal Catering Fees + optional line-item add-ons (beverages, educational services); no clubhouse-extras or food-cost reimbursement mechanism appears. Force-majeure proration is addressed:

> "F. All fees are subject to the Force Majeure provisions set forth in Section VII and the Service Fee, exclusive of the initial installment of the Service (equal to 75% of the Service Fee). Except for the initial installment of the Service Fee, all fees payable hereunder by Club be proportionally reduced and returned to the Club based on the number of days that Catering Service were not provided due to a Force Majeure Event, divided by the total estimate of 240 service days."

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § IV.F p.7]

### B.8 Billing cadence / payment terms

> "A. Each week, the Club will provide Kitchfix a preliminary weekly meal count for the immediately succeeding week. The Club will provide Kitchfix a final meal count at least one day prior to the Monday of the upcoming week. Kitchfix will provide the Club the actual meal count on the day following the day on which Kitchifix provided the Catering Service.
> B. Kitchfix will submit invoices for the Catering Fees in arrears for compensation for the Catering Services every fifteen (15) days and in detail sufficient for a proper pre- and post-audit.
> C. The invoiced amount for the Catering Fees shall be due and payable thirty (30) days from the date of the Club's receipt such invoice. Kitchfix will invoice for the final meal counts provided to them, so long as the actual meal counts have not gone over the projected meal counts. In the scenario where meal counts are over the projected amount, Kitchfix will charge for the additional meals.
> D. Kitchfix will submit invoices for payment electronically to Ashley Meuser at ameuser@reds.com with a copy to sgrossman@reds.com."

[Source: Reds & Kitchfix Signed Agreement-2023 copy.docx § V.A-D pp.7-8]

Service Fee installment schedule: see §B.3 (75% Feb 1 / 25% March 15).
