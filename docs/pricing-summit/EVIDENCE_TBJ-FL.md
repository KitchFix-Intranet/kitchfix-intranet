## §2. Contract evidence (verbatim)

### 2.1 Service Fee (§ 12(a) p.30)

> "Service Fee. Subject to subsections 12(c) and 12(d), in addition to the compensation described above, the Club shall pay to the Provider an annual service fee (the 'Service Fee') in the amount of Four Hundred and Fifty Two Thousand Eight Hundred and Twelve Dollars (USD $452,812), plus applicable taxes, for each SOW #1 Agreement Year during the Term."

### 2.2 Weekly billing cadence (§ 12(e) p.31)

> "Billings and Reconciliations. Within five (5) days following the final day of each Calendar Week (or partial Calendar Week, as applicable) that falls during the SOW #1 Term, the Provider will deliver to the Club an invoice for the amount payable by the Club in connection with the Meals, Snacks and Shakes prepared and served by Provider pursuant to this SOW #1 during the applicable Calendar Week (or partial Calendar Week, as applicable)."

### 2.3 Per-meal rates 2023 base (§ 12(b) p.30)

> "(i) Major League Player Meals. ... $20.29 per Meal, plus applicable taxes (the 'MLB Player Meal Fee').
> (ii) FSL Team Meals - Minor League Regular Season and Postseason Period. ... $14.50, plus applicable taxes, per Meal ...
> (iii) FCL Team Meals – Minor League Regular Season and Postseason Period. ... $10.14, plus applicable taxes, per Meal ...
> (iv) Snacks. ... $1.50, plus applicable taxes, per Snack ... (the 'Snack Fee'), and
> (v) Shakes. ... $5.00, plus applicable taxes, plus applicable taxes, per Shake ..."

### 2.4 Escalation (§ 12(c) pp.30-31)

> "Request to Increase Fees. The Provider will be entitled to increase the price of each Meal Fee and/or the Service Fee on one (1) occasion only during each of the second, third and, if applicable, fourth, fifth, and sixth SOW #1 Agreement Years, subject to a maximum percentage increase equivalent to the percentage increase (if any) identified in the United States federal Consumer Price Index 'Food Away From Home' category ... calculated from the fourth quarter of the calendar year immediately preceding the applicable Agreement Year to the date upon which the Provider submits its price increase request. Notwithstanding the generality of the foregoing, no increase hereunder will be permitted unless:
> (i) each proposed price increase is necessitated solely, exclusively and directly as a result of a documented increase in the Provider's costs to obtain the ingredients or supplies necessary to provide the applicable Meal(s), Snacks and/or Shakes;
> (ii) the Supplier provides the Club with notice by January 31 prior to the applicable Agreement Year ('Price Increase Notice Deadline') ...
> (iii) the Provider is, at the time the price increase is proposed and thereafter, in compliance with this paragraph 12(c)(i), above, and
> (iv) after reviewing the proposed price increase and the supporting evidence and documentation, the Club provides the Provider with written approval of same, which approval will not be unreasonably withheld.
> In addition, for the second, third and, if applicable, fourth, fifth and sixth SOW #1 Agreement Years, to the extent that there is a decrease identified in the United States federal Consumer Price Index 'Food Away From Home' category ... the impacted Meal Fees and Service Fee shall be accordingly deemed decreased for the subsequent Agreement Year."

**Escalation is provider-initiated, requires documented cost basis + Club written approval. Not automatic. Can be a decrease too.**

### 2.5 MFN / Favored Pricing (§ 12(d) p.31) — **VERBATIM**

> "(d) Favored Pricing. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Provider shall make the Meals, Snacks and Shakes available to the Club on terms and conditions, including price, that are at least as favorable as those made available to other Provider customers for substantially equivalent or lower volume purchases. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, in the event that the Provider makes available to any of its customers products that are the same as or substantially similar to the Meals, Snacks and Shakes on terms and conditions, including price, for substantially equivalent or lower volume purchases, that are more favorable than the terms and conditions for the Meals, Snacks and Shakes, as applicable, that are made available to the Club hereunder, then the Provider shall thereafter make available to the Club the Meals, Snacks and Shakes, as applicable, on such more favorable terms and conditions, including price."
